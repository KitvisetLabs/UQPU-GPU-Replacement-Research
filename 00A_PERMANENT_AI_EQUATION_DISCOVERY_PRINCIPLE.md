# PERMANENT AI EQUATION-DISCOVERY PRINCIPLE

**Highest-visibility companion to the Ultimate North Star**  
**Permanent invariant:** INV-035  
**Canonical detail:** `06_AI_ASSISTED_PHYSICS_EQUATION_DISCOVERY.md`

> **The project does not assume that the equations available today are the final possible description of nature. Mathematics and AI may continuously generate new equation hypotheses, effective models and theoretical structures. Imagination is deliberately maximized at the hypothesis-generation stage.**
>
> **But no invented equation becomes physics by declaration. Knowledge, dimensional consistency, established constraints, held-out prediction, falsification, experiment, independent replication, engineering feasibility and end-to-end economics remain mandatory before a hypothesis can support technology.**

This principle exists to keep the project scientifically open without making it scientifically loose.

When an existing physical equation or theorem appears to impose a mission-critical limit, the project shall not simply ignore that limit. It shall either:

1. accept the bound and redesign the technology;
2. quantify engineering headroom within the bound; or
3. formulate a mathematically explicit alternative, identify exactly which assumption changes, derive a discriminating prediction, and let proof/experiment decide.

AI is permanently assigned to help search literature, generate symbolic candidates, test dimensions/symmetries/limits, search counterexamples, design discriminating experiments and record both positive and negative results in GitHub.

**Permanent slogan for the research method:**

> **ULTRA IMAGINATION FOR NEW EQUATIONS; ULTRA KNOWLEDGE AND EVIDENCE FOR REAL TECHNOLOGY.**
