## Fresh literature delta

The new source is Zhang et al., *An AI-Assisted Formalization of the Poincaré Conjecture*, arXiv:2610.08329 (2026-10-06), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08329. The authors report a Lean 4 formalization of the already-established topological and smooth Poincaré theorems, using roughly 90 milestone statements, dependency-aware parallel work, mathematician review of theorem statements, a full Lean build, Comparator checks and an additional Nanoda kernel. They report project-specific figures of about US$25,000 in model/server cost and a development reduced from roughly 3.2 million to 2.8 million uncommented Lean lines after dependency extraction. These are external author-reported values, not UQPU measurements or a general cost estimate.

The novel project-relevant method detail is the separation of (i) statement/assumption fidelity review, (ii) proof construction, (iii) kernel/checker verification, and (iv) independent reproduction. The new watch proposes a matched, small-scale UQPU method test with frozen statements, dependency DAGs, equal resource budgets, an incorrect-statement negative control and mutated proof/log controls. It does not claim that AI discovered the Poincaré theorem or a new physical law.
