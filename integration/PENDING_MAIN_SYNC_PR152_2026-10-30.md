## 1. Objective and method

Add a negative-control regression fixture demonstrating that a high in-sample score for a restricted equation family does not establish that the family is correct. The pure-power-law baseline (y=a x^\mu) is fitted to deterministic synthetic data generated by (y=1+x^2), using training (x=(1,2,4,8,16)). The test asserts log-space (R^2>0.99) while a held-out prediction at (x=32) misses by more than 20%.
