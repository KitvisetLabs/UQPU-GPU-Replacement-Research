## Fresh literature delta

Chung et al., arXiv:2604.21804v3 (revised 2026-10-20), introduce VIBRATO: a bilevel workflow connecting DD4hep/Geant4 full simulation, detector geometry, digitization and reconstruction objectives. The agent proposes candidate geometries and interprets results; deterministic tooling performs simulation, inner optimization and scoring. Three source-reported trajectories each use 48 geometry evaluations.

The study is methodologically relevant to D/G co-design, but its detector-specific model is not a quantum-chip model. The staged search is not a proof of joint global optimality. The reported 2.8 kW ADC figure uses assumed coefficients and is not measured wall-plug power; the source itself calls for device-level calibration. A focused reproduction/transfer gap and D/G interface contract now define provenance, equal-budget baselines, held-out regimes, process constraints and device validation.
