# Lane E — Materials Requirements Derived from Computing

Lane E now consumes requirements from A/C/D/G rather than researching biomass materials in isolation.

## Functional targets
- low-loss dielectric/insulation;
- EMI shielding;
- thermal spreading/interface materials;
- structural packaging;
- adsorbents/purification media for process gases/liquids;
- battery/electrode/storage materials for infrastructure;
- critical-metal recovery media.

## Rule
A biomass-derived candidate advances only when it has a defined incumbent, required property range, expected lifecycle cost and qualification/reliability path.

The research objective is functional substitution/minimization/recovery, not elemental transmutation.
