# Lane H Article 008 — Reproducible Capital Gates

The same discipline used for benchmark certificates can improve long-horizon capital allocation.

A capital gate should identify:
- the decision being tested;
- the reference threshold;
- evidence required;
- capital at risk;
- time to evidence;
- failure condition;
- next option unlocked by success.

For example, quantum hardware CAPEX should not be justified by the statement “the software research is promising.” It should be tied to a quantified blocker and a measurable value unlocked by removing that blocker.

Likewise, biomass/material investments should be tied to a functional-unit advantage, not only low feedstock price.
