## 1. Purpose

Develop a coherent fictional technology family called **Spiritual Communication Mechanics (SCM; กลศาสตร์การสื่อสารทางจิตวิญญาณ)** while preserving the project's evidence discipline. Present physics remains binding for real-world engineering. Religious cosmology, reported anomalous experiences, contested experiments, and fictional mechanisms are kept as distinct epistemic layers.

The framework supports five fictional technology classes:

1. **Spirit/Anomaly Detector (SAD)** — multimodal anomaly detection, not a “ghost detector” by declaration.
2. **Spirit Imaging / Cross-Realm Imager (SRI)** — reconstructs a candidate latent source only after instrumental and perceptual nulls are rejected.
3. **Inter-Realm Communication Terminal (IRCT)** — fictional bidirectional authenticated communication with an independently characterized nonordinary source.
4. **Realm/Spacetime Portal (RSP)** — separate physical-spacetime transport from religious/metaphysical “realm transition”; neither follows merely from quantum entanglement.
5. **Inter-Realm Economic Protocol (IREP)** — only becomes meaningful after identity, agency, communication, accounting, scarcity, enforceability, and conservation constraints are established inside the fictional world.

## 2. Four epistemic layers

- **L0 — Established measurement science:** EEG/MEG, EM/RF spectrum analysis, magnetometry, acoustics, thermal imaging, photon counting, atomic/quantum sensors, time synchronization, cryptographic authentication, statistical inference.
- **L1 — Contested/anomalous empirical literature:** mediumship, NDE reports, EVP/ITC, anomalous information reception. Positive and null results both remain visible.
- **L2 — Explicit fictional hypothesis:** an additional information-bearing degree of freedom or coupling not in the presently validated Standard Model/GR description.
- **L3 — Canon technology:** devices and institutions that become reliable *inside the novel* after fictional experiments establish L2.

Never silently promote L1/L2/L3 to L0.

## 3. Core fictional communication model

Represent a candidate cross-realm channel as

```
Y(t) = H_env[X_env(t)] + H_human[X_human(t)] + H_inst[X_inst(t)]
     + g_SR * H_SR[S(t)] + N(t)
```

where the first three terms are ordinary environment, human, and instrument contributions; `N(t)` is noise; and `g_SR H_SR[S(t)]` is the **fictional** spiritual-realm coupling. Present science corresponds to the null hypothesis `g_SR = 0` until discriminating evidence says otherwise.

A claimed channel must beat:
- preregistered null models;
- shielded/dummy sensors;
- time-shift and location controls;
- blinded human interpretation;
- RF/acoustic/optical/thermal/environmental contamination checks;
- multiple-comparison correction;
- independent hardware;
- independent laboratory replication;
- information-theoretic challenge-response tests.

A dramatic signal is not communication. **Communication requires recoverable, source-dependent information.**

## 4. Device ladder for the novels

### SCM-1 — Anomaly Detector
Sensor fusion: magnetometer/SQUID-class concepts, RF spectrum, infrasound/ultrasound, thermal, ionizing radiation, electric field, vibration, air chemistry, photon counting, EEG only with consent. Output is an anomaly likelihood and provenance record, never “ghost present” by default.

### SCM-2 — Cross-Realm Imager
Uses synchronized detector arrays and inverse reconstruction. The canonical conflict is false imagery from sensor noise, reflections, compression, pareidolia and model hallucination. A valid fictional image requires repeatable geometry, cross-sensor coincidence and blinded reconstruction.

### SCM-3 — Inter-Realm Communicator
Adds a protocol layer: synchronization -> handshake -> challenge -> response -> error correction -> identity/authentication -> semantic decoding. A deceased person's name/voice resemblance is insufficient; the fictional team demands unpredictable challenge-response information unavailable to operators.

Define:
```
R_accept = I_verified / (T * E * C)
```
where `I_verified` is independently verified recovered information, `T` time, `E` energy, and `C` lifecycle cost. This prevents “interesting noise” from becoming a successful telephone.

### SCM-4 — Realm/Spacetime Portal
Two different branches:
- **Physical portal:** speculative spacetime geometry/wormhole branch. Current GR/QFT constraints, stress-energy requirements, quantum energy inequalities and causality remain binding.
- **Realm portal:** fictional extension requiring a new state-space/interaction law. It must specify what conserved quantities cross the boundary, how identity/body state is mapped, and why ordinary detectors do not already see the coupling.

Quantum teleportation/entanglement is **not** a shortcut: ordinary quantum teleportation requires classical communication and does not permit faster-than-light signaling.

### SCM-5 — Inter-Realm Economy
Only after SCM-3/4 fictional validation. Define property/identity, scarcity, energy and information accounting, settlement, fraud resistance, jurisdiction, consent, inheritance and whether physical goods can cross. If only information crosses, the economy trades information/services rather than pretending matter has transferred.

## 5. Falsifiable discovery ladder

For every proposed “spiritual equation”:

**generator -> dimensional/symmetry/conservation/causality constraints -> evaluator -> held-out test -> counterexample search -> simulation/proof -> discriminating experiment -> independent replication -> device consequence -> economics**

Required record:
- incumbent assumption;
- mathematical alternative;
- units/dimensions;
- symmetries;
- conservation rules;
- causal structure;
- limiting/scaling cases;
- null hypothesis;
- falsifier;
- discriminating observable;
- held-out prediction;
- experiment/proof;
- independent validation.

AI-generated equations remain hypotheses.

## 6. Real literature anchors and tensions

- Consciousness science remains unsettled. The 2025 adversarial IIT-vs-GNWT study challenged important predictions of both theories rather than establishing a final theory.
- Quantum-consciousness proposals such as Orch OR exist, but direct evidence for functionally relevant long-lived neural entanglement/coherence remains disputed. Quantum biology is not evidence of post-mortem survival.
- NDEs are real reported experiences; competing neuroscientific and “consciousness beyond brain” interpretations exist. NDE reports alone do not establish an engineered communication channel.
- Mediumship literature contains both positive blinded/meta-analytic results and controlled null results. Treat this as contested evidence requiring replication, not as settled survival evidence.
- EVP is especially vulnerable to auditory pareidolia and expectation/priming; the novel should make this a central experimental adversary.
- Modern single-photon/quantum sensors are genuinely extraordinarily sensitive, but sensitivity to photons/magnetic fields does not make them detectors of spirits.
- Quantum entanglement cannot by itself transmit usable information superluminally.
- Traversable wormholes are mathematical/theoretical research objects subject to severe energy-condition/QFT constraints; they are not demonstrated transport technology.

## 7. Five-volume narrative integration

### Volume 1 — ปฐมกลศาสตร์แห่งจิตและสภาวธรรม
Seed SCM as a **measurement problem**. Characters learn the difference between experience, report, correlation, signal and causal channel. Introduce the null model and the first failed detector. Make failure scientifically productive.

### Volume 2 — บัญชาสวรรค์และกำเนิดอาถรรพ์คอนโทรล
Connect the existing 31-realm cosmology to a **classification system, not a measured coordinate chart**. Build SCM-1/SCM-2. Schumann resonance, extra dimensions and quantum language are explicitly tested rather than treated as magical equivalences. First apparent image fails a blinded control; a later anomaly survives more tests but remains unresolved.

### Volume 3 — มายาการสัทธรรมปฏิรูปและระบบสร้างพญามาร
Make information integrity the battlefield: fake mediums, deepfakes, adversarial AI, cult manipulation, spoofed sensors and false revelation. Develop cryptographic challenge-response and provenance. This volume establishes why an IRCT needs authentication before theology.

### Volume 4 — เปลวเพลิงกลียุคและสงครามชิงโลกธรรม
Move SCM into governance/economics/war ethics. Competing actors attempt to weaponize “messages from another realm.” The protagonists refuse policy or financial settlement based on unauthenticated revelation. Prototype IREP rules are drafted but remain gated on validated agency and channel capacity.

### Volume 5 — รุ่งอรุณสุวรรณภูมิและยุคศิวิไลซ์
Pay off the long arc. Inside the fictional canon, SCM-3 can cross the evidence threshold only after independent replication. SCM-4 remains a harder frontier: communication does not automatically imply transport. The inter-realm economy emerges first as authenticated information exchange; physical exchange requires a separate portal/conservation breakthrough.

## 8. Recommended recurring scene grammar

Every major supernatural-tech scene should pass through:
**experience -> instrument -> alternative explanation -> blinded test -> residual anomaly -> replication -> interpretation -> ethical consequence**.

This lets spiritual meaning and scientific honesty coexist without forcing either to impersonate the other.

## 11. Canon safety rule

Religious concepts may be treated respectfully as religious/philosophical world-models. Scientific language may inspire fictional mechanisms. Neither relationship establishes empirical identity. In-world breakthroughs must be earned through the story's own experiments and cannot be back-projected as real-world evidence.
