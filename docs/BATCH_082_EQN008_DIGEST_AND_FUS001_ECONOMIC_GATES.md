## Fresh literature deltas

1. **DIGEST: Discovery-Guided LLM Tuning for Symbolic Regression**, Jin et al., official arXiv:2610.33880, submitted 2026-10-24: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33880. The preprint reports LLM-derived policy seeding for genetic programming and online feedback from high-quality expressions. It reports up to 2.59% better recovery than the strongest comparator on two ground-truth benchmarks, at least 54.91% relative improvement over LLM-based baselines while using at most 17.4% of their mean search time, and highest accuracy on 122 BlackBox datasets. These are author-reported results; UQPU has not reproduced them and must inspect exact metric denominators, datasets, baselines and resource accounting.
2. **Criteria for the economic viability of fusion power plants**, Whyte et al., *Journal of Fusion Energy* 45, 49 (published 2026-07-10), https://doi.org/10.1007/s10894-026-00577-9. The paper proposes an architecture-neutral economic-gain factor normalized to the energy-capture surface and ten controlling normalized parameters. Its (Q_{econ}\geq1) threshold is necessary but explicitly insufficient for real commercial viability; temporal equilibrium and incomplete cost coverage are important assumptions.
3. **Fusion power experience rates are overestimated**, Tang et al., *Nature Energy* 11, 926–934 (published 2026-03-23), https://doi.org/10.1038/S41560-026-02023-8. Based on technology-characteristic comparisons and expert elicitation, it argues that 2–8% learning rates are better justified than the 8–20% often assumed in projections. This is not a measured commercial-fusion learning curve: commercial plants have not supplied the deployment history needed to estimate it directly.
