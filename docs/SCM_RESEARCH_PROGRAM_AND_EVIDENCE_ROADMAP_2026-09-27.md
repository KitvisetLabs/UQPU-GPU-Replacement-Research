## 1. Program objective

Turn spiritual-communication ideas into a disciplined speculative research architecture suitable for serious fiction without presenting unverified metaphysical propositions as established physics.

The engineering progression is:

```text
reported experience
-> measurable observation
-> ordinary-cause exclusion
-> reproducible anomaly
-> source-dependent information
-> authenticated bidirectional channel
-> transport hypothesis
-> inter-realm system/economics
```

No later stage inherits validity from an earlier stage.

## 2. Evidence states

| State | Meaning | Promotion requirement |
|---|---|---|
| SCM-E0 | religious/philosophical/fictional proposition | none; label explicitly |
| SCM-E1 | anecdote or reported experience | provenance + alternative explanations |
| SCM-E2 | instrument anomaly | calibrated sensor + controls |
| SCM-E3 | replicated anomaly | preregistered protocol + independent replication |
| SCM-E4 | anomalous information | prospective target + leakage-resistant blinded scoring |
| SCM-E5 | bidirectional channel | unpredictable challenge-response + information metrics |
| SCM-E6 | physical mechanism candidate | equation + dimensions + conservation + causality + falsifier |
| SCM-E7 | technology primitive | preparation/control/readout/reliability/scaling |
| SCM-E8 | system/economic evidence | accepted service + lifecycle resource/cost accounting |

Current project status: **SCM-E0/E1 literature study and protocol design.** No SCM-E3+ claim is made by this project.

## 3. Hypothesis registry

### SCM-H001 — Ordinary-environment null
Observed events are explainable by environmental, instrumental, physiological, cognitive or statistical causes.

### SCM-H002 — Residual reproducible anomaly
After controls, a repeatable residual remains but its source is unknown.

### SCM-H003 — Anomalous information channel
A residual carries prospective information unavailable through identified ordinary channels.

### SCM-H004 — Post-mortem-source hypothesis
The information source is a deceased agent. This is strictly stronger than H003 and requires source identity tests that distinguish it from fraud, leakage, inference, living-agent hypotheses and unknown non-agent processes.

### SCM-H005 — Additional physical coupling
A new/effective physical degree of freedom couples the candidate source to measurable matter/fields. Must enter INV-035 equation-discovery governance.

### SCM-H006 — Transport coupling
The coupling supports state/matter transfer rather than information-only correlation. Communication evidence alone cannot promote H006.

## 4. SCM-1 detector gate

Minimum sensor stack for fictional/protocol design:
- synchronized magnetic/electric/RF channels;
- acoustic/infrasound/ultrasound;
- thermal/visible/near-IR imaging;
- vibration/pressure/environmental channels;
- photon-counting route where justified;
- human physiology only with consent and separate causal interpretation.

Required controls: dummy sensors, shielded channels, randomized active/inactive rooms, time-shift controls, known interference injection, calibration pulses, operator blinding, immutable timestamps and raw-data retention.

Primary output is **anomaly provenance**, not a spirit label.

## 5. SCM-2 imaging gate

Imaging requires multi-sensor spatial coincidence and independent reconstruction. Reject candidates that depend on:
- compression artifacts;
- reflections/lens flare;
- thermal persistence;
- sensor saturation/dead pixels;
- model hallucination;
- human pareidolia;
- post-selection from many frames.

A fictional SCM-2 promotion requires a held-out location/time prediction and blinded reconstruction above preregistered null performance.

## 6. SCM-3 communication gate

Communication is an information-theory problem.

Use randomized challenge payloads generated after operators and candidate communicators are isolated. Score exact or preregistered semantic recovery against a defined null.

Candidate metrics:

```text
BER = erroneous recovered bits / transmitted challenge bits
I_verified = independently verified source-dependent information
R_verified = I_verified / elapsed_time
C_accept = lifecycle_cost / accepted_verified_information
```

Identity authentication must use multiple unpredictable challenges. Voice, face, writing style, names and biographical facts alone are insufficient because they may be copied, inferred or leaked.

## 7. SCM-4 portal gate

Separate:

1. **physical-spacetime transport** — constrained by established GR/QFT and causality;
2. **fictional realm-state transport** — requires an explicit new state space and coupling law.

Required questions: what crosses; what remains conserved; mapping of body/information/identity; energy and entropy accounting; causal structure; return path; failure mode; observable distinguishing transport from reconstruction.

No entanglement-only or quantum-teleportation shortcut is permitted.

## 8. SCM-5 economic gate

An inter-realm economy requires validated agency plus a transfer channel. Model information-only exchange before matter exchange.

Required contracts: identity, consent, ownership, scarcity, settlement, fraud resistance, jurisdiction, inheritance, channel capacity, energy/cost per transaction and conservation/accounting for anything physically transferred.

## 10. Literature delta

### 2026 retrospective mediumship case analysis
PubMed PMID 41923690 reports a historical/recording-based analysis of Chico Xavier and Isidoro Santos and interprets a substantial subset of identified items as difficult to explain conventionally, favoring a survival interpretation. **SCM classification: contested retrospective evidence; not prospective authenticated communication and not project evidence.**

### Controlled literature remains mixed
Published blinded/triple-blind studies and a meta-analysis report above-chance anomalous-information results, while other strictly controlled experiments and forced-choice work report null/insufficient evidence. Therefore SCM does not select a metaphysical conclusion from this literature. The research consequence is stricter prospective challenge-response, leakage analysis, preregistration and independent replication.

### Quantum sensing
Modern quantum sensors can exceed classical measurement limits for specified physical quantities. Their sensitivity is useful to SCM-1 instrument design, but sensitivity to known fields is not evidence that a spiritual source exists.

## 11. Failure registry

Treat these as first-class negative evidence:
- signal disappears under blinding;
- effect follows experimenter expectation;
- anomaly follows RF/acoustic/thermal contamination;
- classifier succeeds only after post-selection;
- effect fails independent hardware/lab replication;
- biographical information is discoverable before the trial;
- channel cannot answer unpredictable prospective challenges;
- proposed equation violates units/conservation/causality without an explicit replacement theory;
- claimed portal is only remote communication or reconstruction.

## 12. Next highest-value experiments

**SCM-GATE-001 — Multimodal null-characterization dataset.** Record synchronized ordinary environments, deliberate interference and dummy sensors. Goal: quantify false-positive behavior before any paranormal claim.

**SCM-GATE-002 — Pareidolia/blinded-decoder benchmark.** Compare human and AI interpretations of noise/anomalous audio/image samples with labels withheld.

**SCM-GATE-003 — Prospective information protocol.** Design a cryptographically timestamped challenge-response experiment with precommitted scoring and leakage audit. Protocol design does not assume a spirit source.

**SCM-GATE-004 — Independent replication contract.** Freeze hardware/software/data schema so a second team can reproduce a positive or negative result.

Only after a robust SCM-E4/E5 result should equation discovery attempt to fit a new coupling mechanism to data.

## 13. Five-volume canon transfer

- **Volume 1:** SCM-GATE-001 and failed/ambiguous detection establish scientific discipline.
- **Volume 2:** SCM-1/2 and the struggle against sensor/perceptual artifacts.
- **Volume 3:** SCM-GATE-003, identity/authentication, deepfakes and false revelation.
- **Volume 4:** replication, governance, weaponization, law and prototype information economy.
- **Volume 5:** fictional E5/E6 breakthrough may unlock IRCT; portal transport remains a separate higher gate; economy begins with authenticated information exchange.

This transfer is narrative architecture, not evidence that the fictional breakthroughs exist in the real world.
