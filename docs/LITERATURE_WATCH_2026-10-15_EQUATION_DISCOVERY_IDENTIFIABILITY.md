# Literature Watch — Equation Discovery, Identifiability and Lower Bounds

**Accessed:** 2026-10-15  
**Project evidence class:** `LITERATURE_REVIEW / THIRD_PARTY_PREPRINT`  
**Promotion boundary:** none of these sources is project experimental evidence, a new physical law, a hardware result or an economic result.

## Material delta

Three October 2026 equation-discovery preprints materially reinforce the repository's fail-closed method.

1. **LLM-IDEA** ([arXiv:2610.11253](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11253), submitted 2026-10-15) places an identifiability diagnostic outside the language model and distinguishes a model that is not yet solved from one that the available experiment class cannot identify. Its reported limitations include a protocol/prompt confound and shared memory across seeds, so its p-values are not imported as project evidence.
2. **RISR** ([arXiv:2610.11387](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11387), submitted 2026-10-15) ranks residual-informed correction actions and evaluates them on held-out observation clouds, unseen product actions and higher-dimensional inputs. Its held-out utility rows are separate from coefficient fitting but not from every synthetic-problem construction step. This is useful methodology, not proof of law recovery on physical data.
3. **Coupled Harness–Model Evolution** ([arXiv:2610.17740](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17740), submitted 2026-10-15) separates fit, selection and externally scored test data and evolves executable tools before policy training. It motivates treating the harness and the candidate equation as separately versioned experimental factors.

A new quantum-complexity result, **Lower Bounds for Preprocessing Attacks on Quantum Cryptography** ([arXiv:2610.02101v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02101), 2026-10-03), proves time–space–success bounds in a quantum random-oracle setting. It is relevant to FND-002 and resource accounting, but its cryptographic oracle model does not lower-bound the project's optimization, AI, memory or storage workloads and is not evidence for or against GPU replacement.

## Concrete project decisions

- Cycle 008's `AI-COST` gate now requires distinct training and held-out hashes and complete quality/runtime/energy/cost fields. A fit without this separation is rejected.
- `FND/EQN` requires explicit quantity kinds, unit codes and exact dimension vectors before an equation can pass even a structural balance check.
- A future `EQN` candidate packet will add an **identifiability verdict** (`IDENTIFIABLE`, `LOCALLY_NON_IDENTIFIABLE`, or `EXPERIMENT_CLASS_EXHAUSTED`) generated by deterministic code, plus the null direction or exhaustion certificate where applicable.
- Harness version, candidate version, fit split, selection split and untouched test split must receive independent hashes. The test split cannot select the final equation.
- Time/space/query lower bounds may constrain only workloads that satisfy their stated computational model and assumptions.

## Falsifiable follow-up

Use a synthetic ODE fixture with a deliberately confounded parameter pair. Freeze two observation protocols: one whose sensitivity matrix has a known null direction and one that breaks it. The repository gate must (1) recover the rank/null direction, (2) reject an equation selected with test leakage, (3) preserve exact units and dimensions, and (4) return `EXPERIMENT_CLASS_EXHAUSTED` only when every declared design in the bounded class retains the null direction. This remains `MODEL_ONLY / SYNTHETIC_TEST` until independent data and replication exist.
