The authors describe the fast-pyrolysis bio-oil range as roughly twice conventional fuel benchmarks in their comparison.

Only the publisher-linked laboratory metadata/abstract was reviewed; the full article, supplementary data, cost year, workbook, financial assumptions and uncertainty model were not reconstructed. These are third-party TEA outputs, not observed market prices or project evidence. The article does not establish Pangola feedstock transfer or a saleable finished fuel at those prices.
