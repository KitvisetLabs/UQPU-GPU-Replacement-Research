# Lane H Article 004 — Option Value of Software-First Quantum Research

A long-horizon industrial plan benefits from experiments that are inexpensive relative to the capital commitments they can prevent or justify.

For UQCS, software-first cloud-QPU research has **option value**: a benchmark can reveal whether a workload maps poorly to current quantum hardware before the project commits to custom chip, fab or factory investment. Conversely, a reproducible workload advantage would identify which hardware bottleneck is worth financing.

A simple research-capital rule is therefore:

**fund uncertainty reduction before irreversible scale.**

This does not stop the hardware, biomass, photonics or fabrication lanes. It changes when very large capital commitments become justified.

Suggested gate metrics include reproducibility, output quality, total cost/useful-task, data-movement burden, provider portability, hardware blocker specificity, and the cost of the next experiment.

**Evidence status:** STRATEGIC_RESEARCH / MODEL_ONLY. It is a capital-allocation research framework, not investment advice.
