## Source

Martinez-Villarreal et al., “Multiobjective Optimization of the Hydrothermal Liquefaction of Microalgae for Cost-Effective Advanced Biofuels Production,” *Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research* 65(27), 14446–14460; published online 2026-06-30; DOI 10.1021/acs.iecr.6c00395.

## Genuine relevance

The publisher abstract reports a scale-dependent temperature/residence-time optimum and lower modeled MFSP than yield-only optimization: 5.16% lower at the small-capacity optimum and 19.27% lower at the upscaled optimum, with up to 4.50% lower HDO hydrogen demand. The reported MFSP values remain high (13.04 €/L and 3.77 €/L), and the study reports significant economic uncertainty. The useful lesson is methodological: yield, quality, upgrading demand and scale should be optimized jointly.

## Blocking limitations

- Microalgae are not Pangola or the local agricultural-residue feedstock.
- Full paper, supplementary information, response surfaces, data, experimental design, process model and cost assumptions have not been independently audited.
- The abstract does not provide enough information to reconstruct feedstock basis, full mass/energy boundary, accepted final-fuel specification, hydrogen/catalyst life, utilities, CAPEX/OPEX or uncertainty.
- No local delivered-feedstock cost, Pangola yield, commercial fuel price or project financing data are supplied.

## Unlock criteria

1. Retrieve and hash the paper and free supporting information; reconstruct the experimental design, objective weights and response surfaces.
2. Separate measured observations from fitted response surfaces, process simulation and TEA outputs.
3. Reproduce MFSP and hydrogen-demand changes from the stated cost and process assumptions.
4. Validate the optimization on held-out conditions and an independently measured feedstock lot.
5. Run matched Pangola HTL/pyrolysis comparisons under one accepted-liquid specification; close all liquid, aqueous, gas and solid streams.
6. Include preprocessing, water handling, upgrading, hydrogen, catalysts, utilities, CAPEX/OPEX, logistics, financing, rejected product and non-double-counted coproduct credits. Report credit-free and credited cases separately.
