## Thesis

A long-horizon industrial plan should allocate capital against independently accepted evidence milestones, not against aspirational multipliers or the apparent size of a technology's theoretical state space. The strategic sequence remains agriculture and biomass → advanced materials and energy → semiconductor/photonics/quantum infrastructure → AI/robotics and future industries. Each transition needs a measurable technical function, full cost boundary, and a dated decision gate.

The US$10T–US$100T North-Star figure is an ambition-scale proxy. It must be translated into measurable service/capability, energy, physical-compression and lifecycle-cost contracts. A phone that only terminals into remote infrastructure is not the physical-compression outcome.

## Evidence-informed capital gates

1. **Reproducibility first.** Fund source pinning, data provenance, independent tests and small exact references before expensive hardware or pilot commitments.
2. **Software before hardware where scientifically appropriate.** Lane A establishes useful-output semantics and competitive CPU/GPU/NPU baselines; B confirms provider/API/target constraints; C measures state, I/O and memory movement. A simulator pass is not a hardware result.
3. **Device/manufacturing co-design.** D specifies device and packaging requirements; G demonstrates that a real process/equipment chain can meet process windows, metrology, yield and cost-per-good-device requirements. D/G should not be funded as disconnected roadmaps.
4. **Materials and energy pilots only after accepted-function closure.** E must demonstrate measured feedstock, mass/energy balance, product quality, lifetime and utility requirements. Bio-oil must be priced per accepted final liquid-energy function, not raw bio-crude yield. Fusion must be priced per reliable net delivered electricity, including recirculating power, availability, replacement, maintenance, financing and decommissioning.
5. **Scale-up only after an independent customer/service and lifecycle-cost case.** F owns matched baselines, uncertainty, full cost/task or cost/accepted-MJ accounting, and evidence promotion. H translates accepted evidence into the next capital tranche.

## FIN-001: separate principal, interest, time and rates

For a debt tranche with unchanged coupon, fees, term and repayment schedule, reducing financed principal reduces absolute interest cashflows. This is a direct accounting identity that must be verified against the actual amortization schedule. It does not prove a lower coupon, lender spread, internal hurdle rate, WACC, economy-wide rate or project risk premium.

Each tranche ledger should separately record:
- required, committed, drawn and outstanding principal with dated drawdowns;
- coupon, fees, compounding convention, repayment schedule and realized interest;
- equity amount, dilution and required return;
- collateral, asset composition, guarantees, subsidies and covenant constraints;
- project and innovation risk, downside/failure cases and stage-gate decisions;
- capital lock-up duration, construction/development duration and time-to-evidence;
- cashflows and cost per independently accepted milestone.

Compare debt/equity/hybrid only under matched milestone cashflows and technical-risk assumptions. Retain rejected and failed projects to avoid survivor bias. Report principal-only counterfactuals separately from rate/WACC/hurdle-rate hypotheses.

## Decision rule

A tranche is released only when its predecessor gate has:
1. a frozen useful-output or accepted-product contract;
2. source/data/code provenance sufficient for independent reproduction;
3. a competitive baseline and explicit uncertainty;
4. full resource and lifecycle-cost accounting;
5. a falsifier and stop/continue threshold;
6. an identified owner and a dated next decision.

If a gate fails, preserve the negative evidence and reduce or redirect the next tranche. A shorter project timeline is not automatically a lower risk premium; measure capital lock-up and cashflow effects directly.
