## 5. Bosonic-QEC source ledger

Putterman et al., “Hardware-efficient quantum error correction via concatenated bosonic qubits,” Nature 638, 927–934 (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-08642-7, is the selected literature anchor. The source describes concatenated cat-coded storage with four transmon syndrome ancillas, two-photon dissipative stabilization, a 2.8 μs QEC cycle and MWPM decoding. It reports an overall logical error per cycle of 1.65(3)% at |alpha|² = 1.5.

The project has not frozen a target logical error or retention-cycle horizon. The paper's logical-error result is not a project measurement, a logical lifetime, a complete physical-component error budget, or evidence of UQPU advantage. Preparation, control/stabilization, readout, decoding wall time, energy and cost are still unfilled.

## 6. Pangola materials and fabrication gate

ASTM D4935-18R26 is the named planar-material shielding method for normal-incidence/far-field measurement over 30 MHz–1.5 GHz. The proposed protocol uses 101 log-spaced points including endpoints, retains raw VNA S data, calibration/fixture identity, mounting orientation, uncertainty and a blinded randomized run order.

Parker Chomerics PREMIER A230-HTHF is the proposed incumbent. Its datasheet's typical density (1.39 g/cm³) implies 2.085 kg/m² at 1.50 mm; the same datasheet reports typical shielding averages of 60–70 dB in the D4935 band. These are manufacturer-reported typical values, not same-lot project measurements. The incumbent must be remeasured with the candidate under the same protocol.

The proposed trial recipe is 800±5°C under N₂, 10°C/min ramp, 60-minute dwell, N₂ cooling, <150 μm char, and 40 wt% char / 60 wt% epoxy, with three batches of five coupons. Drying to constant mass and recording moisture/ash are planned. Exact resin/hardener SKU and cure schedule, authenticated Pangola lot/species and access to the full method text are unresolved. The code's 2% geometry relative tolerance is a project screening decision only; its suitability must be checked against actual metrology uncertainty before any physical comparison. No feedstock processing, coupon fabrication or testing occurred in this delta.
