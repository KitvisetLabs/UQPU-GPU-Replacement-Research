# Lane H — Ketskaew Chulamani / Kanusanan Pongpanna Model Research Roadmap

Lane H converts the permanent master plan into a living sequence of falsifiable strategic research questions and public articles.

## Research sequence
### H1 — Cash-flow foundation
Research Pangola grass/agricultural/biomass businesses by unit economics, logistics, processing yield, market risk, working capital and automation potential.

### H2 — Biomass -> higher-value materials
Map feedstocks into carbon materials, cellulose/lignin products, adsorbents, electrodes, composites, recovery materials and industrial chemicals. Connect candidates to Lane E/D/G requirements.

### H3 — Financing ladder
Study staged financing: operating cash flow, customer-funded growth, equipment finance, bank credit where suitable, startup equity, strategic investors, grants/R&D partnerships and project finance. No financing route is assumed available without diligence.

### H4 — Automation / One Person Business Company
Test which business processes can be automated safely with Local AI, Local Deep Agents and connected agent systems, while retaining human governance for payments, legal/compliance, security, hiring/firing, material contracts and consequential decisions.

### H5 — Future-industry expansion
Define technical/economic gates for moving from materials/energy to semiconductor/photonics/quantum, then AI/robotics, biotech, food/water, cities/infrastructure and space industry.

### H6 — Speculative horizon
Keep warp-drive/stargate-like concepts as SPECULATIVE research questions separated from engineering roadmaps until evidence changes.

## Publication rule
Each Lane H article should state:
- thesis/question;
- evidence level;
- current evidence;
- assumptions;
- economics;
- dependencies;
- risks;
- falsification/unlock criteria;
- links to the permanent multilingual master-plan sources.
