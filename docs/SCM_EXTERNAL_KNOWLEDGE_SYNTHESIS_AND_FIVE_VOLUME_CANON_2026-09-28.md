## 3. Evidence map
### Established measurement layer
Quantum/atomic sensors can measure very small known physical quantities such as light and electromagnetic fields. Single-photon detectors have finite dark-count/error behavior; higher sensitivity therefore increases the need for calibration and null characterization, not permission to label weak events “spiritual.”

Quantum teleportation transfers quantum information using entanglement plus classical communication; it does not teleport matter and is not a demonstrated bridge to religious/spiritual realms.

Traversable wormholes remain theoretical and are strongly constrained by semiclassical gravity/quantum energy inequalities. They can inspire the physical-spacetime portal branch but are not an engineering recipe.

### Contested/anomalous-information layer
Experimental mediumship literature is mixed. Blinded/triple-blind studies and a meta-analysis report above-chance information in some datasets, while a forced-choice study reports insufficient statistical evidence. Even positive anomalous-information findings do not uniquely identify a deceased source.

EVP-like ambiguous audio is vulnerable to pareidolia and priming. This directly motivates SCM-GATE-002.

### Consciousness/NDE layer
A 2025 neuroscience review proposes an integrated neurophysiological/psychological model of NDEs. Published correspondence disputes whether neurocentric models capture all phenomenology, and a separate narrative review argues for a beyond-brain interpretation. The repository therefore treats NDEs as an unresolved research domain, not proof of a realm channel.

Modern consciousness theory remains unsettled; adversarial testing of IIT and GNWT is useful primarily as a model of how competing theories should make risky, discriminating predictions.

## 5. Technology ladder
### SCM-1 — Anomaly/Spirit Detector
Real substrate: calibrated multimodal sensing. Fictional breakthrough: a residual signal survives controls and predicts held-out events. Device UI should show provenance and competing ordinary causes, not a binary GHOST lamp.

### SCM-2 — Cross-Realm Imager
Real substrate: photon/thermal/optical arrays plus sensor fusion. Fictional breakthrough: spatially coherent reconstruction repeats across independent sensor modalities and sites. AI may reconstruct only from frozen raw data; hallucination/pareidolia benchmarks are mandatory.

### SCM-3 — Inter-Realm Communication Terminal
Real substrate: information theory, cryptographic commitments, authentication and error correction. Fictional breakthrough: unpredictable challenge-response produces reproducible source-dependent information. The terminal evolves from text/bit exchange to audio, then video-like reconstruction; “video” may be decoded state reconstruction rather than ordinary photons traveling from a realm.

### SCM-4 — Realm/Spacetime Portal
Branch 4A: physical spacetime shortcut inspired by GR/wormhole research but constrained by energy/causality. Branch 4B: fictional realm-state portal requiring a new state space and coupling law. Communication success cannot automatically promote transport. Matter, energy, entropy, identity and return-path accounting are separate requirements.

### SCM-5 — Inter-Realm Economic Protocol
Start with authenticated information services, not matter. Only after a transport primitive exists can physical commodity transfer be contemplated. Every transfer needs conservation/resource accounting.

## 8. Inter-realm economy and gold
Gold is useful narratively because it exposes the difference between **information about ownership** and **physical transfer of matter**.

Three fictional stages:
1. **Gold claim/ledger:** no atom crosses; authenticated parties exchange claims or settlement information.
2. **Custodied cross-realm claim:** each realm maintains independently audited reserves and a settlement bridge.
3. **Physical gold transfer:** only after SCM-4 demonstrates a conservation-accounted matter channel.

Required accounting for stage 3:
`M_in - M_out - M_stored = measurement_error + explicitly modeled loss`.
If mass/energy appears without a balance law, the portal model fails rather than becoming “free gold.”

Economic plot risks: counterfeit identity, double-spending claims, asymmetric scarcity, inheritance by deceased agents, sanctions/jurisdiction, arbitrage, information asymmetry, channel congestion, energy cost and monopoly over portal infrastructure.
