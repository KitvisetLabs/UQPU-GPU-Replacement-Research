# Lane H Article 003 — Stage-Gate Finance from Biomass Cash Flow to Deep Technology

The Kanusanan Pongpanna Model spans businesses with radically different capital intensity and technical risk. A practical financing architecture therefore needs gates rather than assuming that profits from one stage automatically justify the next.

## Gate structure

**Gate 1 — Cash-flow proof.** Demonstrate repeatable unit economics, customers, operating discipline and reinvestable surplus in the current business.

**Gate 2 — Research option.** Fund small experiments whose failure is affordable but whose success opens a materially larger opportunity.

**Gate 3 — Prototype evidence.** Increase capital only after technical milestones, cost models and integration requirements are measurable.

**Gate 4 — Pilot economics.** Validate throughput, yield, reliability, market demand, compliance and financing structure at pilot scale.

**Gate 5 — Scale finance.** Use the financing instrument appropriate to the risk: retained earnings, project finance, strategic partners, equity, grants, debt or combinations thereof.

## Portfolio rule
The long-horizon vision remains intact, but capital is released progressively. Speculative technologies remain research options until evidence changes their status.

## UQCS connection
Quantum programming/cloud experiments are especially valuable early because they can falsify or support core computing hypotheses before committing to custom fabrication CAPEX. Chip/factory investment remains active research but should scale when software experiments expose a hardware bottleneck that has a quantified economic value.
