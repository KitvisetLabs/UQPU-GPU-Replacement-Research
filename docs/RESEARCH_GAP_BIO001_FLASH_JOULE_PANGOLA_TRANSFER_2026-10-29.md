# Research gap RG-BIO001-FLASH-JOULE-PANGOLA-TRANSFER-001

**Opened:** 2026-10-29  
**Owner:** Lane E; D/G process co-design; Lane F evidence/economics  
**Status:** LITERATURE LEAD / PUBLISHER FULL TEXT NOT AUDITED / PANGOLA TRANSFER NOT DEMONSTRATED

Flash-Joule heating records in *Renewable Energy* 263 (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.renene.2026.125516, and *Energy* 364 (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.energy.2026.142520, suggest that rapid electrical thermal pulses can alter biomass product distribution and produce candidate char/gas streams. Metadata and indexed abstracts were found, but the primary publisher pages/full data were not accessible in this check; this gap is therefore a provisional literature lead.

**What is missing:** source-data provenance; measured wall-plug energy; complete dry-feed mass balance; liquid-water/oxygen/stability and accepted-fuel quality; upgrading/hydrogen/catalyst burden; continuous yield and uptime; scale-up CAPEX/OPEX; life-cycle boundaries; Pangola-specific feedstock properties; independent replication.

**Unlock:** retrieve and pin primary articles/data, then compare against a matched conventional-pyrolysis baseline using the same feedstock and accepted liquid-energy specification. Include char and gas credits only once and only at defensible saleable value. No commercial bio-oil, low-cost energy, or universal carbon-material substitution claim is permitted.
