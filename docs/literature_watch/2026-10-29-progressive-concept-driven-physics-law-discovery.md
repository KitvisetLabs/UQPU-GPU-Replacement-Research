# Literature watch — progressive and concept-driven equation discovery

**Date checked:** 2026-10-29  
**Primary owners:** Lane A (reproducible evaluation contract) and cross-cutting INV-034/035  
**Evidence class:** PUBLISHER-ACCEPTED PAPERS + OFFICIAL PREPRINT METADATA; no UQPU reproduction  
**Decision:** adopt as benchmark-design inputs only; no equation is promoted to a physical law.

## Sources and dated status

1. **Mingkun Xia and Weiwei Zhang, “Data-driven progressive discovery of physical laws.”** *Physical Review Research*, accepted 2026-10-06; official publisher page: https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/accepted/10.1103/f2kp-pp4q ; preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13727 .
2. **You-Le Fang, Dong-Shan Jian, Xiang Li and Yan-Qing Ma, “AI-Newton: A concept-driven physical law discovery system without prior physical knowledge.”** *Physical Review Research*, accepted 2026-10-09; official publisher page: https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/accepted/10.1103/dvvc-4rsr ; preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01538 .
3. **Ho Fung Tsoi et al., “Machine Can Automatically Discover Parametric Functions to Model HEP Data.”** Official arXiv record, version 2 revised 2026-10-19: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19750 .

## Genuine delta

The two recent journal-acceptance records emphasize complementary search structures: CoSR assembles laws through successive, physically interpretable intermediate relations; AI-Newton proposes concepts from multiple experiments and then tests whether those concepts generalize across a broader set. The HEP SymbolFit work is a useful adjacent example of repeated symbolic search with uncertainty modeling, but its fitting task is not itself discovery of a new fundamental law.

For UQPU, the methodological implication is to evaluate the discovery process, not just the final training fit. Candidate methods should be compared on identical raw records, experiment/group holdouts, complexity, physical-constraint violations, predictive uncertainty, counterexample yield, reproducibility and total search cost. Staged discovery is a hypothesis about better generalization—not a universal guarantee.

## Limitations and evidence boundary

- Publisher acceptance establishes a publication milestone, not independent replication by this project.
- Rediscovering Newtonian relations from mechanics experiments is a method check against known laws, not evidence that current physics is false or that a new equation exists.
- Symbolic fit, concept naming and cross-experiment generalization do not alone establish causality, dimensional consistency, conservation, uniqueness or a new physical law.
- The HEP example targets background/function modeling in collider analyses; it must not be conflated with fundamental-law discovery.
- No cited paper demonstrates UQPU speedup, quantum memory, new material performance, Pangola-carbon substitution, accepted bio-oil, net fusion electricity or reduced financing rates.

## Falsifiable follow-up

1. Freeze a small known-law corpus with units, symmetries, conservation constraints and noisy multi-experiment observations.
2. Use identical training data for one-shot symbolic regression, staged composition and concept-driven search; reserve whole experiment families and parameter regimes for blind holdout.
3. Score held-out error, complexity, calibration, dimensional/symmetry violations, counterexample rate, false-discovery rate, compute/search cost and sensitivity to noise.
4. Include null and deliberately misspecified systems to measure false discovery; retain every failed candidate.
5. Require a preregistered discriminating observable and independent data before any candidate is treated as a physical-law candidate. No AI output is a law by declaration.
