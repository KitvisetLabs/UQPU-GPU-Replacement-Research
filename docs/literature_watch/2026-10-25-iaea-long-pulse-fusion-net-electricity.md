## Genuine delta

The EAST conference contributions describe dynamic lithium-powder wall conditioning combining feedforward injection settings with feedback from in-situ Li-II line emission. The authors report a 605-second H-mode discharge under controlled recycling/impurity conditions and discuss its use in support of a 1066-second steady-state H-mode result. A separate contribution reports a 1066-second fully non-inductive H-mode with lower-hybrid input around 0.92 MW, electron-cyclotron input around 1.9 MW and about 3.05 GJ of cumulative injected RF energy.

The CFEDR baseline-design abstract reports integrated-model predictions of fusion power around 2.9 GW, plasma gain Q=30 and net electric output above 500 MWe at a design duty factor of 0.5, with repetitive pulses exceeding one hour. The same abstract identifies unresolved helium de-enrichment versus tritium retention, tungsten penetration from plasma-facing-component erosion and runaway-electron damage to the vessel.

## Limitations and evidence boundary

- These are official conference abstracts/presentations, not a new independently reproduced UQPU result. The 1066-second EAST discharge is described in cited work as a 2025 operational milestone; this meeting is a recent technical update, not evidence that the discharge occurred for the first time during the meeting.
- Pulse duration is not plant capacity factor. Injected RF energy is not saleable electricity, and plasma gain Q is not electric gain.
- The CFEDR net-power value is an integrated-model projection, not measured plant export. No complete auxiliary-load ledger, CAPEX, construction schedule, lifecycle cost or financing terms were established by these abstracts.
- The abstracts do not provide enough source data to independently recompute the full net-electric boundary.

## Exact UQPU relevance

- **INV-032 / FUS-001:** add long-pulse control, lithium consumption, fuel recycling, high-Z impurity accumulation, heating/current-drive power and unresolved fuel/material issues to the evidence checklist. Do not infer low-cost electricity.
- **INV-033:** longer pulses may inform utilization and maintenance hypotheses, but no principal, interest, construction-time or WACC reduction is measured.
- **Lanes D/G:** lithium powder delivery, tungsten components, erosion, coating durability and heat-removal windows need measured qualification.
- **Lane F:** net electricity must be gross electrical conversion minus recirculating plant power, with availability, maintenance, fuel-cycle, cooling, grid and financing boundaries explicit.
- **FND-001 / INV-035:** operational signals are not entropy-production or fundamental-physics measurements.
- **INV-029–031:** no phone-class physical compression, Pangola-carbon substitution or accepted bio-oil result follows.

## Proposed falsifiable follow-up — FUS-LPO-001

1. Pin the conference contributions, referenced papers and presentation files by URL, date and source hash; distinguish abstract-level numbers from values in primary papers.
2. Reconstruct the EAST ledger: pulse duration, LH/EC input, coupling/current-drive efficiency, wall-conditioning mass/flow, recycling, tungsten concentration, auxiliary systems and uncertainties. Treat 3.05 GJ as injected RF energy, not net output.
3. Obtain the CFEDR full power balance and assumptions for conversion, recirculating power, duty factor, availability, tritium breeding, component replacement, maintenance, cooling, grid, decommissioning and financing.
4. Calculate uncertainty-bounded net MWh and lifecycle cost only when each input has traceable evidence. Falsify any economic transfer if auxiliary loads erase export margin or maintenance/fuel-cycle constraints dominate availability.

**Pass condition:** a source-bound, independently checked power/availability ledger closes on the same net-electric boundary and reports uncertainty.  
**Failure condition:** key loads, availability or fuel/material constraints remain unbounded, or the claimed margin disappears under sensitivity analysis.  
**Promotion boundary:** literature lead only; no commercial fusion, net-electricity-cost or new-physics result.
