## Claimed result and assumptions

The preprint studies a restricted class of scale-invariant scalar–tensor models with non-minimal derivative coupling to the Einstein tensor, using effective-field-theory-of-dark-energy stability conditions. It reports that phantom crossing requires an effective-coupling sign conflicting with its no-ghost condition, and presents two further obstructions that rely on swampland de Sitter/distance conjectures.

The first obstruction is a mathematical stability constraint within the specified action and assumptions. The swampland-based arguments depend on conjectural quantum-gravity criteria and must not be described as experimentally established laws.
