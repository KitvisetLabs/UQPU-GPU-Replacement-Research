## Material delta

The authors present a direct path-integral calculation of the chiral anomaly in an adiabatic regime for a massless fermion on electromagnetic and curved backgrounds, including local temperature and chemical potential. They report additional quantum-thermodynamic contributions to axial-symmetry breaking that induce chiral-separation and chiral-vortical effects.

## Genuine novelty and boundary

The useful novelty for this project is a newly published, technically relevant QFT treatment connecting anomaly calculations with local thermodynamic fields and curved backgrounds in the stated adiabatic regime. This is a theoretical extension/calculation within quantum field theory, not a claim that the established anomaly framework is experimentally invalid, not a new elementary particle, and not evidence of a new physical law.

The paper was received 2025-08-07, accepted 2026-09-28 and published 2026-10-20. The fact that it is newly noticed in this cycle must not be confused with a result discovered today by UQPU.
