1. AWS Quantum Technologies Blog, 2026-10-21, “Decoding quantum errors with NVIDIA Ising Decoding on AWS.”

## 1. AWS: NVIDIA Ising Decoding on accelerated instances

Official source: https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/quantum-computing/decoding-quantum-errors-with-nvidia-ising-decoding-on-aws/ (published 2026-10-21).

AWS describes training an AI-based pre-decoder on EC2 P4d/P5/P5en/P6/G6e instances, with eight GPUs per instance, using a shared containerized workflow. The reported training data are generated by Stim from a distance-9 surface-code memory experiment under a circuit-level depolarizing model at physical error rate p = 0.006; each epoch contains about 67 million shots. The source reports up to roughly 39% lower training time from A100-based P4d to B200-based P6, and reports cost/time trade-offs among instance types using AWS US East (N. Virginia) on-demand prices as of October 2026. Its simulated distance-31 inference comparison reports roughly 2.4x–2.9x lower decoder latency per round for the tested pre-decoder-plus-MWPM configurations versus MWPM alone, while improving the reported logical error rate.

**Evidence boundary:** this is an official technical report of GPU training and validation on simulated syndromes/noise, not a UQPU replication or proof of end-to-end advantage on a real fault-tolerant QPU. Provider-reported performance and prices must be independently rechecked before a cost model is used.

**UQPU relevance:** quantum error correction may impose a classical real-time latency budget. A QPU workflow that depends on an external GPU decoder has not replaced that GPU's useful function; the GPU/decoder cost, memory, data movement, host orchestration and latency must remain in the full-stack comparison. The decoder may be a legitimate baseline or co-processor, not an omitted free service.

## 2. Quandela: GPU–photonic-QPU integration via NVQLink

Official source: https://www.quandela.com/about-us/newsroom/gpu-qpu-integration-nvidia-nvqlink/ (published 2026-10-13).

Quandela describes a hybrid workflow connecting an NVIDIA DGX Spark to its FPGA-based quantum system controller through NVIDIA NVQLink and CUDA-Q. The showcased GTC Berlin QPU itself is simulated; the company says the architecture builds on earlier experimental validation of a low-latency integration path with a physical photonic QPU at ISC High Performance. The article does not provide a complete independently audited end-to-end latency/cost dataset.

**Evidence boundary:** distinguish the simulated public demonstrator from the company's stated earlier physical-QPU integration validation. Neither establishes a useful-work advantage, general-purpose GPU replacement or a Bell-QSL-compatible sensing target.

**UQPU relevance:** provider-neutral lowering must account for controller placement, host/GPU/QPU communication, payloads, synchronization, batching, reconstruction and queueing. Photonic gate-model hardware is not automatically equivalent to the bosonic continuous-variable sensing setup in the Bell-QSL literature.

## 3. Southeast Asian provider availability signal

Official source: https://www.quandela.com/about-us/newsroom/ (announcement dated 2026-10-22).

Quandela and Quanova announced a plan to deploy a MosaiQ photonic quantum computer at Quang Trung University in Vietnam, alongside training and research infrastructure. The announcement describes a planned deployment and ecosystem program; it is not a current UQPU-accessible endpoint, published price sheet, or completed hardware acceptance test.

**UQPU relevance:** keep Southeast Asian quantum-provider discovery active, but do not add a usable provider adapter or claim local hardware availability until endpoint access, modality, SDK, queueing, target identity, pricing and authorization are verified.

- **E — energy/materials/infrastructure:** no direct biomass, bio-oil, fusion or energy result; source/pump, GPU and facility energy remain in any later full-stack comparison.
- **F — economics/evidence:** provider prices are time- and region-specific; compare the same useful output, noise model, logical error target and billing boundary.

- **H — strategic publication:** record regional ecosystem developments as market/access context, not as scientific or commercial performance evidence.

## Falsifiable follow-up

1. Pin the public Ising Decoding implementation, Stim version, container and training configuration.
2. Reproduce the simulated distance-9 training and distance-31 inference results independently, recording seeds, logical error rate, latency per round, GPU model, memory, energy if measurable, and full instance-hour cost.
3. Compare MWPM, pre-decoder+MWPM and eligible alternatives at identical syndrome/noise distributions and accepted logical error targets; retain the regimes where the pre-decoder loses.
4. For any NVQLink/photonic adapter, require target identity, physical-versus-simulated mode, controller topology, payload size, round-trip latency distribution, failure/retry behavior and complete cost accounting.
5. Do not submit paid QPU work without credentials, bounded budget and explicit user consent.
