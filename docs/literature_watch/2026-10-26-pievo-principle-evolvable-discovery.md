**Watch date:** 2026-10-26

**Owners:** Lane H (source/publication), Lane A (test contract/runtime), Lane F (evidence and economics)

## Genuine novelty

PiEvo shifts part of scientific-agent search from selecting hypotheses inside a fixed prior to maintaining and expanding a compact set of candidate principles. Its published method combines information-directed hypothesis selection using Gaussian-process reasoning with anomaly-triggered principle augmentation and a multi-agent loop for principle proposal, hypothesis generation and experimental evaluation. The authors report results across four discovery benchmarks, including up to 29.7–31.1% improvement over their selected baselines and up to 83.3% faster convergence in steps. These are paper-reported results for the authors' tasks and setup, not UQPU measurements.

The official code repository is public and documents model/API configuration, dependencies and surrogate-model options. UQPU did not install or run PiEvo, call a model API, reproduce its benchmarks or inspect every benchmark's underlying data. This is a method lead, not a verified UQPU performance result.

## Limitations and failure modes

- Reported improvements depend on the paper's four benchmark definitions, baselines, evaluation metrics, model backends and resource budgets; cross-paper headline percentages are not comparable without a matched experiment.
- Expanding a principle space after an anomaly can become post-hoc accommodation. The test set must never trigger principle edits or tune the final claim.
- A principle generated by an agent is still a hypothesis. Better search efficiency cannot replace dimensional, symmetry, conservation, causality, limiting-case, proof, counterexample, experimental and independent-validation gates.
- LLM tokens, model/API fees, surrogate evaluations, failed runs, retries, human time and data preparation belong in total search-cost accounting. Missing price or energy is unavailable, not zero.
- No new physical law, quantum advantage, QPU, material, bio-oil, fusion-electricity, financing or Data-Center-to-Phone result follows from this publication.

## Exact UQPU relevance

- **EQN-001/EQN-006 and FND-006:** candidate generators may expand their principle vocabulary when a frozen training/validation anomaly justifies it, while knowledge and evidence remain the promotion authority.
- **Lane A:** test whether principle-space expansion helps recover known equations or improve held-out prediction under the same compute and search budget as a static-principle baseline.
- **Lane F/H:** count the entire discovery process and preserve the source-to-claim audit trail. A faster search is useful only if accepted, reproducible formulas are obtained at lower total cost under a named cost basis.
- **Lanes C/D/G:** only a separately validated equation with a concrete device or state/data-movement consequence may trigger downstream co-design.
- **Lanes E/F:** later application to biomass conversion, constitutive/material models or fusion cost drivers must use domain-specific data and physical units; the discovery framework itself is not evidence for yield, substitution or net electricity.

## Falsifiable follow-up: EQN-PIEVO-001

1. Pin the paper/code commit, dependency environment, task definitions, model configuration, datasets and their hashes before execution.
2. Begin with synthetic ground-truth equation families. Compare a static-principle search with a principle-expanding search on identical initial observations, candidate evaluator, seeds, wall-clock budget, model/token budget and total compute budget.
3. Pre-register the anomaly trigger using training/validation data only. Seal held-out and extrapolation splits; forbid principle updates after any test observation.
4. Score exact symbolic recovery where available, held-out and extrapolation error, complexity, dimensional/symmetry/conservation/causality violations, counterexamples, false discoveries, failures, retries, memory, CPU/GPU time and cost per independently accepted formula.
5. Use at least 20 preregistered seeds or justify another sample-size rule before seeing outcomes. Keep failed runs in the denominator and report uncertainty, not only the best run.
6. Falsify a claimed benefit if it disappears under matched resources, relies on test leakage, increases false discoveries, fails held-out/extrapolation tests, violates constraints or cannot be independently reproduced.
7. Only after this gate passes may a domain-specific physical dataset be considered. Any challenge to established physics must identify the changed incumbent assumption, alternative equation, discriminating observable and experiment/independent validation path.
