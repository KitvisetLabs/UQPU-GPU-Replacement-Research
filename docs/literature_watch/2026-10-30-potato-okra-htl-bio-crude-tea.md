# Literature watch — 2026-10-30 — potato–okra HTL–AD–HDO bio-crude and TEA comparator

**Source:** Abdulrazaq Magaji Hussaini, Habibu Abubakar Waniyo, Ahmed Mohammed Inuwa, Saeed Isa Ahmed, Kabir Garba and Idris Misau Muhammad, “Process simulation and techno-economic assessment of hydrothermal liquefaction of binary potato–okra food waste for green fuel production using Aspen Plus,” *Chemical Engineering Journal: Green and Sustainable*, vol. 3 (2026), article 100080, September 2026.

**Evidence class:** PEER-REVIEWED ARTICLE / PROCESS SIMULATION + TECHNO-ECONOMIC SCENARIO / MODEL_ONLY_EXTERNAL.  
**Primary owner:** Lane E — biomass/bio-oil process; Lane F — economics/evidence; D/G — process equipment and scale-up.

## Reported result and genuine relevance

The study models a binary potato–okra food-waste route integrating hydrothermal liquefaction (HTL), anaerobic digestion (AD) of aqueous/organic residual streams and hydrodeoxygenation (HDO) upgrading. The publisher abstract reports:

- simulated bio-crude yield increasing from 18.35 wt% at 200 °C to 36.9 wt% at 320 °C, with bio-crude HHV rising from 27.8 to 33.2 MJ/kg;
- a reported HTL bio-crude energy-recovery ratio (ERR) of 66.58%;
- an integrated AD result of 0.77 m³/kg VS biogas with 62.1% methane at 58 °C;
- a modeled integrated-system NPV of about US$11.2 million, IRR about 15–16%, and payback of 6.56 years.

The useful transfer is a process-boundary checklist for a wet-residue HTL–AD–HDO alternative to dry-feed pyrolysis. It reinforces that raw biocrude is not the accepted final fuel: upgrading, aqueous-stream treatment, hydrogen, catalysts, AD, utilities, CAPEX/OPEX and financing must all be included.

## Critical limitations and consistency checks

1. **Feedstock transfer:** potato/okra market waste is not Pangola grass or the project's local residue stream. Its ash, alkali, nitrogen, moisture, collection cost and gate-fee economics cannot be transferred.
2. **Simulation boundary:** the paper describes model validation against external literature datasets; the potato–okra HTL outputs are predictive simulation results, not an independent experimental campaign on that feedstock. The reported RMSE/model-validation claims must be reproduced before reliance.
3. **Product basis:** distinguish the optimized 36.9 wt% bio-crude case from the separate integrated-process stream table reporting 31.6 wt% bio-crude. Distinguish HTL-only ERR (66.58%) from integrated HTL–AD–HDO energy recovery claims. Confirm dry-feed versus slurry basis and all stream boundaries.
4. **Financial reconciliation:** the text reports deterministic NPV near US$11.24 million but also describes a Monte Carlo mean near US$2.79 million, while nearby sensitivity ranges are materially higher. Reconcile the base case, probability distributions, figure, supplementary assumptions and cash-flow calculation before using any TEA number.
5. **Scenario specificity:** the model uses approximately 1,242 kg/h biomass, an 8:1 water-to-biomass ratio, 8,000 h/year, Nigeria-specific Q4 2025 prices and a negative-cost/gate-fee feedstock assumption. These are not Pangola logistics or current project prices.
6. **No net-energy or commercial inference:** an HHV-based product ERR is not net plant efficiency. External hydrogen, heat, electricity, water treatment, compression, AD/HDO utilities, downtime, replacement, financing and decommissioning require full accounting.

## Exact BIO-001 / INV-031 relevance

Use this paper only as an external HTL pathway comparator and a list of missing cost/energy streams. A Pangola route must still optimize accepted liquid-energy function per delivered feedstock and full lifecycle cost, including preprocessing, moisture, yield, upgrading, hydrogen/catalyst, utilities, CAPEX/OPEX, logistics, financing, rejected product and non-double-counted coproducts. Hydrochar, biogas and other coproduct credits require distinct stream IDs and incremental-cost accounting.

It does not establish Pangola bio-oil, commercial low-cost fuel, net energy gain, carbon-negative performance, critical-material substitution or any UQPU project economics.
