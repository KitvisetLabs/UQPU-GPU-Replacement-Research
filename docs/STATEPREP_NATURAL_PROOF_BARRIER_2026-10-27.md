## Fresh literature delta

Christine Li and Natalie Parham's official preprint, “Natural proofs for quantum state preparation lower bounds,” arXiv:2610.06826v1, submitted 2026-10-05, establishes a conditional barrier against a class of natural-property proof strategies. The full theorem uses a classically subexponentially secure pseudorandom-function assumption with polynomial-size, depth-d threshold-circuit implementations and targets a fixed level of the Magic Hierarchy. Under the paper's stated Naor–Reingold corollary assumption, the level is 52.

The genuinely new UQPU contribution is not the external theorem. It is a fail-closed method contract that keeps largeness, constructivity, circuit-class scope, superpolynomial target and the cryptographic assumption explicit. A barrier classification restricts a proof strategy only; it never automatically rejects a theorem claim.
