# Lane E — Material Evidence Ladder

A biomass/advanced-material candidate progresses through:

1. FUNCTION HYPOTHESIS;
2. SOURCED PROPERTY EVIDENCE;
3. FUNCTIONAL-UNIT COMPARISON;
4. PROCESS-COMPATIBILITY EVIDENCE;
5. RELIABILITY EVIDENCE;
6. PILOT COMPONENT;
7. QUALIFIED SUBSTITUTE / COMPLEMENT.

Raw-material price alone cannot advance a candidate beyond step 1.

The project may also conclude that a biomass route is better for recovery, shielding, packaging, purification or infrastructure rather than direct replacement of a high-conductivity metal.

**Evidence status:** ENGINEERING/RESEARCH GOVERNANCE.
