## 3. Integrated findings

1. The Ising comparison can no longer pass as equivalent when the instance hash, accepted output, error tolerance or confidence differs.
2. A bosonic logical-error result alone is insufficient: the ledger exposes the preparation/control/readout/decoder and cost/energy inputs required by the project’s full-resource gate.
3. Coupon differences are descriptive only after the Pangola candidate and incumbent have matched thickness, areal density and frequency points.
4. SCM package consistency verifies hashes and protocol invariants. It does not test whether a spiritual source exists or support a cross-realm claim.
5. Provider cost is a direct subtraction from the fixed AI-COST target. Until a quote or authorized billing record exists, the allowable residual share stays unknown.
6. Both capital records leave money unbudgeted and unauthorized; passing a software check cannot release funds.

The existing literature snapshot remains the source anchor for biomass-derived EMI materials and published bosonic-QEC experiments. This delta creates schemas and tests; it does not add Pangola measurements, bosonic hardware evidence, provider runs, independent SCM data, or an AI cost result.
