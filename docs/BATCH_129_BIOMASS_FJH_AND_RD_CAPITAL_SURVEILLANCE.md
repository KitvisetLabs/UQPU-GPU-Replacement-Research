1. **BIO-001 / INV-030:** Song et al., *Renewable Energy* 263 (2026), 125516, DOI 10.1016/j.renene.2026.125516, reports feedstock- and temperature-dependent product partition under flash-Joule heating across six crop straws. Pan et al., *Energy* 364 (2026), 142520, DOI 10.1016/j.energy.2026.142520, reports a flash-Joule route to nitrogen-doped bamboo biochar and H2/CO-rich gas. The useful delta is process-intensification/selectivity to investigate, not proof of low-cost accepted fuel or Pangola transfer. See the [biomass watch](literature_watch/2026-10-29-flash-joule-biomass-conversion.md) and [focused gap](RESEARCH_GAP_BIO001_FLASH_JOULE_PANGOLA_TRANSFER_2026-10-29.md).
2. **FIN-001 / INV-033:** Guenzel & Liu, “Excess Commitment in R&D,” *Review of Financial Studies* 39(7), 2179–2221 (2026), published 25 March 2026, DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhag026, reports delay-associated continuation and crowd-out in pharmaceutical clinical-trial R&D. The project-relevant hypothesis is to precommit stage gates and measure capital lock-up/time-to-evidence without letting sunk costs alone justify continuation. It does not establish lower UQPU WACC or generalize automatically. See the [finance watch](literature_watch/2026-10-29-rnd-capital-lock-in-stage-gates.md) and [focused gap](RESEARCH_GAP_FIN001_RD_DELAY_CAPITAL_LOCKIN_2026-10-29.md).
