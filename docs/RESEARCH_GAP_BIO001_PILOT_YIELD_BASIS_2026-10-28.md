These are not Pangola measurements. The pilot study's 0.94 CNY/kg minimum-selling-price scenario depends on process and char-recovery assumptions; the ANN's reported fit is not evidence of temporal/feedstock-family/scale generalization.

## Required closure

1. Pin sources, available data/code/license, feedstock and reactor conditions.
2. Reconcile mass-yield and volumetric liquid rates using dry-feed basis, moisture, liquid density and full mass balance.
3. Close energy balance across liquid, char, gas, water, heat input/recovery and losses.
4. Measure accepted product quality and any required stabilization/upgrading, hydrogen/catalyst demand, utilities, downtime and final energy yield.
5. Compute full saleable-liquid cost including CAPEX/OPEX, logistics, financing and non-double-counted coproduct credits.
6. Use source-pinned chronological, feedstock-family and reactor-scale holdouts for predictive models; compare with a transparent baseline and independent measurements.

**Non-claims:** no commercial ultra-low-cost bio-oil, Pangola-derived material performance, universal carbon substitution or elemental transmutation is demonstrated.
