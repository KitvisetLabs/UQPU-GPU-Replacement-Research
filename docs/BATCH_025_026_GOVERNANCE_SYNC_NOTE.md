## D098 — AI may invent equations; evidence decides physics

The project permanently distinguishes **equation-hypothesis freedom** from **physical-claim freedom**.

Mathematics, humans and AI may generate alternative equations, effective laws, mathematical structures and theoretical models without being limited to currently used formula families. Present experimentally supported physics remains binding for present engineering claims until a candidate alternative survives the relevant mathematical, predictive, falsification, experimental and independent-validation gates.

A current impossibility bound cannot be bypassed merely by saying future physics may change. The correct escalation contract is:

`incumbent assumption -> proposed mathematical change -> changed prediction -> discriminating observable -> proof/experiment -> independent validation -> engineering/economic consequence`.

This decision is published at root level in `00A_PERMANENT_AI_EQUATION_DISCOVERY_PRINCIPLE.md` and `06_AI_ASSISTED_PHYSICS_EQUATION_DISCOVERY.md` and is CI-protected by `test_ai_assisted_equation_discovery_is_permanent`.
