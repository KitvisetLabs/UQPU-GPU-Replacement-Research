## Objective

Test whether a discovered equation remains predictive across grouped held-out physical systems and semantics-preserving representations, rather than winning only on one split, one notation or one residual pattern.

## New source leads

- LegoSR (arXiv:2610.33465, posted 2026-10-24) proposes a shared Equation IR and traceable multi-solver experiment environment with an adaptive agent.
- RISR (arXiv:2610.11387, posted 2026-10-08) uses pointwise residual structure to guide formula proposals and correction selection.
- UQPU has not reproduced either preprint. Their benchmark results are external evidence only; neither establishes a new physical law.

See the source-specific article at docs/literature_watch/2026-10-29-legosr-risr-equation-discovery-robustness.md.

## Implementation in this cycle

Added a dependency-free metric helper to validate disjoint group splits and compare matched baseline/candidate held-out errors across at least three named cases. The metric gate requires lower mean candidate/baseline error, limits worst per-case regression, and blocks promotion when any transformation is unverified. SHA-256 fields preserve provenance references only; they do not verify semantic equivalence.

Regression tests cover split disjointness, duplicate IDs, matched-case identity, improvements, severe per-case regressions, unverified transformations, invalid digests, invalid numbers and minimum-case requirements.

## Blockers

- No external solver code/checkpoints, model weights or benchmark datasets were pinned or reproduced.
- No project-specific equation dataset has been frozen with group-disjoint held-out regimes.
- No equation candidates, pointwise errors, uncertainty intervals, counterexample results or energy measurements were generated by this cycle.
- The helper does not parse equations, check dimensions, prove equivalence or establish physical truth.
- Material, biomass, fusion and finance datasets still require domain-specific measurements and uncertainty.

## Required unlock criteria

1. Pin source revisions, code/checkpoints, licenses, dependencies, data provenance and compute budgets.
2. Create synthetic exact-ground-truth cases and freeze group-disjoint equation-family/system splits before tuning.
3. Compare static SR, search-controller, residual-guided and shared-IR multi-solver paths under equal candidate and compute budgets.
4. Record units, symmetries, conservation, causality, limiting cases, complexity, interval calibration, held-out ID/OOD errors, counterexamples, false discoveries and failed runs.
5. Independently verify transformations and re-run the chosen result; for physical interpretations, specify a discriminating observable and experiment.
6. Only then connect a candidate to device, material, BIO-001, FUS-001 or FIN-001 consequences.

## Falsifiers / stop conditions

The method fails if the average gain disappears on grouped holdouts, any transformation is semantically invalid, worst-case regression exceeds the preregistered limit, independent runs do not reproduce the ranking, or evaluation cost exceeds the value of the discovery.

## Non-claims

No external dataset reproduction, new physical law, theorem about nature, real-QPU advantage, universal subsystem replacement, Pangola-carbon substitution, accepted low-cost bio-oil, net fusion electricity, lower WACC or phone-class physical-compression result is established.
