# RG-BIO001-SCWL-TWO-STEP-001 — Pangola one-step vs two-step SCWL

**Owner:** Lane E  
**Interfaces:** D/G (catalysts/process equipment), F (mass-energy economics), H  
**Status:** LITERATURE_ONLY / PANGOLA_EXPERIMENT_NOT_RUN  
**Opened:** 2026-10-30  
**Source:** *Energy & Fuels* 40(29), 15858–15879 (2026), DOI 10.1021/acs.energyfuels.6c01592.

## Objective

Test whether a 30-minute subcritical-water pretreatment followed by supercritical-water liquefaction improves accepted liquid-fuel function per dry Pangola feedstock and total lifecycle cost relative to a matched one-step SCWL control.

## Evidence and blocker

The peer-reviewed 2026 article compares one-step and two-step SCWL across lipid-, carbohydrate- and protein-rich biomass groups. Publisher full text/supplement was inaccessible in this cycle; no numerical yield or cost is transcribed. The study is not identified as Pangola-specific. Feedstock composition, ash, moisture and downstream fuel upgrading must be measured.

## Required next gate

Retrieve and pin full paper/supplement and reconstruct the source protocol. Then run randomized matched Pangola batches with independent lots and one-step/two-step controls. Close mass/carbon/energy balances through accepted fuel, including H2, catalysts, utilities, water recycle, wastewater, char/gas, CAPEX/OPEX, logistics, financing and non-double-counted coproduct credits.

## Falsifiers

Reject if pretreatment energy/water costs erase accepted-fuel gain; carbon recovery declines; upgrading/hydrogen needs increase; final liquid fails specification; or independent-batch uncertainty shows no meaningful improvement.
