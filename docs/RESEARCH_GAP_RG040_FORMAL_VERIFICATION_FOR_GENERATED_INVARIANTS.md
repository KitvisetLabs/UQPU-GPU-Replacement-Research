**Primary owner:** Lane A — quantum programming/workloads/compiler/runtime

## Objective

Determine whether candidate invariants generated by AI can reduce the human effort needed to verify selected provider-neutral compiler/runtime transformations or small hardware-control properties without weakening correctness. The relevant method lead is Peled et al., IJCAI 2026, “Large Lemma Miners: Can LLMs Do Induction Proofs for Hardware?” The paper's 109-task, 90%-at-least-one-prompt result is limited to its own RTL benchmark and does not transfer automatically to UQPU.

## Current state

- Lane A has existing QSP/QSVT feasibility, reconstruction and fail-closed comparison gates; this gap does not replace them.
- The equation-discovery contract requires assumptions, constraints, scaling cases, falsifiers, observables and independent validation plans.
- The new BIC regression tests that candidate-count ranking remains unchanged under common positive RSS rescaling, as required when the same residual observations are expressed in different measurement units.
- No formal proof assistant or SMT proof has yet been integrated into the UQPU compiler path under this gap.
- No device RTL, fabrication tool, physical experiment or provider workload is validated by the literature source.

## Unlock criteria

1. Freeze a small rewrite or controller transition system, its property, assumptions and version/hash before candidate generation.
2. Preserve every generated lemma, malformed output, solver rejection, counterexample and timeout.
3. Prove initiation, consecution and implication to the stated property with a pinned formal checker.
4. Compare against a manually authored invariant and measure success rate, reprompt count, proof runtime, human repair time and resource use on the same task set.
5. Check specification faithfulness and independently reproduce at least one accepted proof/model.
6. Keep a held-out task set; do not tune prompts or thresholds on held-out outcomes.
7. If extending to a physical equation, separately require dimensional and symmetry checks, conservation/causality compatibility, limiting cases, held-out predictions and a discriminating experimental path.

## Failure semantics

A counterexample rejects the current candidate or exposes an assumption defect. A solver timeout is TOOL_BLOCKED, not proof of infeasibility. A formally proved property under an unvalidated specification is not a physical result. Model-count selection, proof success and symbolic-regression fit must not be treated as evidence of a new physical law.
