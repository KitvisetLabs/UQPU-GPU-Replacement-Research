# Lane G — Quantum Chip Manufacturing Equipment & Software Roadmap

Lane G owns the production-machine/software stack, not the qubit architecture itself.

## Factory stack
1. substrate/wafer preparation and handling;
2. lithography: mature optical, DUV, immersion DUV, EUV where justified, direct-write, e-beam, nanoimprint and modality-specific patterning;
3. deposition: PVD/CVD/ALD/epitaxy and modality-specific films;
4. etch/material removal;
5. doping/implantation/thermal processing where applicable;
6. cleaning/surface preparation;
7. metrology/inspection/defect control;
8. electrical/optical/cryogenic characterization;
9. bonding/assembly/2.5D/3D/packaging;
10. automated process control, SPC/APC, traceability, EDA/TCAD/process simulation and factory robotics.

## Rule
Choose the least-cost equipment/process route that satisfies the device requirement. Do not default to EUV.

## Current evidence
The architecture is FABRICATION_PROPOSAL / MODEL_ONLY until calibrated against real device processes, equipment specifications, yields and factory economics.
