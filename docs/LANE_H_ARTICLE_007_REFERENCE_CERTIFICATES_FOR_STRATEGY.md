# Lane H Article 007 — Reference Certificates for Strategy

The benchmark work suggests a broader governance lesson: important strategic claims should be attached to a reference standard rather than marked successful because an activity was completed.

Examples:
- a business pilot compares realized cash conversion and unit economics with a predefined gate;
- a material candidate compares against an incumbent functional unit;
- a quantum workload compares against a competitive classical baseline;
- a factory process compares capability against a device requirement window.

This turns the long-range plan from activity tracking into evidence-gated progress.
