## 5. Logical-memory service contract and source boundary

The project-chosen falsification screen requires one logical qubit to survive 100 syndrome cycles with total logical failure probability ≤0.05, with basis-resolved acceptance and a frozen decoder/erasure policy. Under an identical stationary independent-error model, the corresponding per-cycle limit is `1-(0.95)^(1/100) = 0.0005128014`; the union-bound sufficient limit is 0.0005.

Putterman et al. report a 1.65 ± 0.03% overall logical error per cycle at `|α|²=1.5` and a 2.8 μs cycle. Using only the central value, the simple independent model gives 0.810575 total failure across 100 cycles and a 32.176 ratio to the independent limit. The comparison says only that the source central value does not meet this project-chosen screen under either simple bound. It does not reinterpret the paper's uncertainty, reproduce its experiment, set a published threshold or describe a project device.

The associated Zenodo record was resolved as dataset version v1, published 2024-12-03, with `data_upload.zip` displayed as 145.5 MB and MD5 `d4f051ba40bf3d1940f90f9da4e9953c`. Preview access was rate-limited and the archive was not ingested; no raw-data claim is made.
