## Objective

Extend FIN-001 to distinguish interest-rate changes from credit-availability/drawdown/covenant shocks in milestone financing.

## Evidence and blocker

The paper's abstract reports stronger sensitivity of R&D-intensive Indian firms to monetary tightening and bank credit-supply shocks using firm-level data for 2002–2024. Full publisher text/tables were inaccessible in this cycle. India estimates are not project- or Thailand-specific and do not establish a project WACC change.

## Required next gate

Retrieve the full article and identification/robustness tables. Add separate rate-only, credit-only and joint scenarios to the milestone tranche ledger. Record principal, committed limits, drawdown dates, interest, spreads, fees, maturity, grace, collateral, covenants, working capital, time-to-evidence and delays. Use actual counterparty terms when available; otherwise label ranges MODEL_ONLY.

## Falsifiers

Reject a finance benefit if it only shifts principal between entities, excludes fees/working capital, ignores delayed drawdown or assumes lower WACC from lower principal without separate rate/risk-premium evidence.
