## 1. Purpose
Run multiple independent research directions concurrently while preventing a result in one lane from being misrepresented as evidence for another. Parallelism is for search breadth and throughput, not weaker standards.

## 3. Shared evidence gates
Every lane uses the same ladder:
```text
question -> baseline -> model/proof/simulation -> adversarial/null test
-> reproducible artifact -> external/independent evidence
-> device/system consequence -> economics -> verified-discovery gate
```
A simulation cannot satisfy a measured-hardware gate. A theoretical possibility cannot satisfy manufacturability. A physical effect cannot satisfy economic advantage without lifecycle accounting. SCM fictional success cannot satisfy real-world evidence.

## 4. Current parallel work packets

Reconcile QOS/QSVT synthesis work with first verified workload wins and AI-COST functional equivalence. Produce negative results when synthesis/resource overhead closes the claimed advantage.

### P-B — Real provider evidence
Prepare provider-matched execution/billing packets without assuming authorization or inventing hardware results. Freeze exact workload/output/cost contract first.

### P-C — State/memory compression
Continue semantic memory replacement and output-information lower bounds. Identify tasks where compact observables avoid full-state reconstruction.

### P-D — Device + sensing
Connect active-qubit calibration/device requirements to spin-defect, photonic, superconducting or other measurable primitives. SCM sensors may borrow metrology methods but not source interpretation.

### P-E — Pangola/biomass materials

### P-F — Energy + finance + economics
Maintain fusion/bio-oil/interest-cost as scenario research until evidence improves. Integrate energy, cooling, maintenance, financing and uncertainty into accepted-output economics.

### P-G — Factory
Choose least-cost fabrication route satisfying device requirements. Link process variables to metrology, error/yield and cost; do not default to EUV.

### P-H — Strategic commercialization
Map cash-flow foundation -> materials -> financing -> semiconductor/quantum expansion. Capital follows cheapest decisive falsification/unlock experiment.

### P-FND/EQN — Deep frontier
Run lower-bound, alternative-primitive, QFT/particle mechanism and mathematics-first invention scans in parallel. New equations remain hypotheses until INV-035 criteria pass.

### P-SCM — Spiritual Communication Mechanics

## 5. Dependencies
- A/B/F form the **useful-output economics triangle**.
- C constrains A through state preparation/readout and output size.
- D/E/G form the **device-material-factory triangle**.
- FND/EQN can feed A/C/D/E but cannot bypass their engineering gates.
- H consumes verified outputs from all lanes for capital sequencing.
- SCM can reuse D sensing, C information bounds, A protocol/compiler concepts, F economics and G metrology; it cannot inherit their evidence for a spiritual source.
- AI-COST depends on A/C/B/F and eventually D/G for hardware reality.

## 6. Cadence
Run a portfolio cycle rather than a single linear queue:
1. one cheapest decisive experiment/model per active lane;
2. CI/reproducibility check;
3. evidence-state update;
4. cross-lane dependency update;
5. kill/continue/escalate decision;
6. only verified discoveries enter root `articles/`.

## 7. Promotion rule
Parallel execution never means simultaneous promotion. Each lane advances only when its own evidence gate passes. Negative/null results remain first-class outputs and may terminate expensive branches early.
