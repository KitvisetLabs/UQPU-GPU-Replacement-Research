The peer-reviewed *Review of Financial Studies* paper “Excess Commitment in R&D” (Guenzel & Liu, 2026; DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhag026) reports delay-associated continuation and crowd-out effects in pharmaceutical clinical-trial projects. It motivates a falsifiable governance hypothesis for UQPU research: when evidence is delayed, decisions may overweight sunk cost and underweight future opportunity cost.

**Blockers:** external validity to this project; complete UQPU project-level spend/timeline/decision data; counterfactual portfolio outcomes; lender terms and financing principal; separation of delay-caused information from sunk-cost bias.

**Unlock:** maintain a milestone ledger with initial hypothesis, remaining cost, time-to-evidence, uncertainty reduction, precommitted stop/continue rules, alternative use of capital, and documented exceptions when delay supplies new information. Evaluate project-level principal/interest/time-to-evidence separately from WACC or macro-rate claims.
