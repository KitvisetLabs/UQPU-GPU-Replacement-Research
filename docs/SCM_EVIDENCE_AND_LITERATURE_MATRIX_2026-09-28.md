## 1. Reading rule
A paper's authors may favor a survival, anomalous-information, neurophysiological or other interpretation. This matrix records the reported result separately from the interpretation. No single study is promoted directly into SCM mechanism or canon fact.

Evidence labels:
- **ESTABLISHED-SUBSTRATE:** validated measurement/information technology.
- **CONTESTED-POSITIVE:** study reports an anomalous effect but source/mechanism remains disputed.
- **NULL/CONSTRAINING:** study fails to establish the proposed anomalous effect or exposes a false-positive route.
- **OPEN-THEORY:** active scientific disagreement.
- **FICTION-BRIDGE:** permitted inspiration only, not real-world evidence.

## 3. What each evidence family can and cannot establish
**Mediumship accuracy** can motivate a test of source-dependent information. It cannot by accuracy alone distinguish deceased agency from leakage, inference, fraud, living-agent hypotheses or unknown processes.

**EVP/perceptual research** establishes that ambiguous signals plus expectation can create meaningful-seeming perceptions. It does not prove every anomalous recording is pareidolia; it requires blinded decoding and signal provenance.

**NDE research** supplies competing brain/consciousness hypotheses and measurable physiological variables. NDE report alone is not a coordinate measurement of another realm.

**Quantum sensing** supplies measurement sensitivity and metrology. “Quantum” is not a semantic bridge from a small signal to a spiritual source.

**Quantum information** supplies authentication/capacity/error-correction analogies. Entanglement does not eliminate ordinary causal/classical requirements in known teleportation protocols.

## 4. SCM technology mapping
### SCM-1 Detector
Primary literature inputs: quantum/atomic magnetometry, photon metrology, environmental sensing, pareidolia controls. Promotion target: reproducible residual anomaly after ordinary-cause modeling.

### SCM-2 Imager
Requires calibrated optical/thermal/photon arrays + multimodal spatial coherence + blinded reconstruction. AI output is downstream interpretation, never raw evidence.

### SCM-3 Communicator
The strongest lesson from contested mediumship is to replace retrospective resemblance with prospective information: low-leakage target -> cryptographic commitment -> frozen response -> exact/precommitted scoring -> replication -> identity challenges.

### SCM-4 Portal
Must be a separate mechanism program. Information-channel evidence cannot establish transport. Known quantum teleportation is an explicit anti-analogy for matter portals.

### SCM-5 Economy
Begins at authenticated information and identity. Physical gold exchange remains locked behind a conservation-accounted transport primitive.

## 5. Five-volume canon mapping
**Vol 1:** teach epistemic layers through failed detector/null experiments; consciousness theories appear as competing research programs.

**Vol 2:** sensor engineering + EVP/pareidolia crisis. The first “ghost image/voice” fails blinding; later residual anomaly remains unresolved.

**Vol 3:** mediumship-inspired information experiments become cryptographic challenge-response. Deepfake/AI/metadata leakage are antagonists equal to supernatural uncertainty.

**Vol 4:** institutions fight over what evidence threshold permits action. Positive and null laboratories coexist; replication provenance becomes political/legal infrastructure.

**Vol 5:** fictional E5 bidirectional authentication may finally be reached. Characters explicitly distinguish this in-world breakthrough from historical ambiguous evidence. Portal and physical-economy programs remain higher gates.
