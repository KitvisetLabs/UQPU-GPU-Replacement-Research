# Research gap RG050 — magnetized-target fusion temperature-to-net-electricity translation

**Date opened:** 2026-10-26  
**Status:** OPEN — external experimental diagnostic milestone; no integrated net-electricity or cost evidence  
**Primary owner:** Lane E — materials, energy, cooling and infrastructure  
**Cross-lane owners:** Lane D — diagnostics/device interfaces; Lane G — equipment/maintainability; Lane F — net-electric cost and finance; Lane H — literature publication  
**Related:** FUS-001; INV-030–033; INV-034/035  
**Literature watch:** docs/literature_watch/2026-10-26-general-fusion-mtf-1kev-net-electric-boundary.md

## Objective

Determine which measured magnetized-target-fusion (MTF) plasma and driver parameters can be responsibly propagated into the architecture-neutral FUS-001 net-electric cost-driver envelope. Prevent a temperature milestone from being treated as a Lawson-criterion result, net fusion energy, electricity export or a commercial cost estimate.

## Current external evidence

The UKAEA/GOV.UK announcement dated 2026-10-08 and the associated General Fusion technical-paper page report Thomson-scattering measurement of electron temperature at **1090 ± 40 eV** on the LMC-12 compression shot. The diagnostic was described as measuring at eight times and two spatial points. The technical paper was reported as submitted for peer review, not as independently replicated here.

The technical paper describes a collapsing solid lithium liner in the measured shot; the broader commercial concept is described as using a liquid-metal wall. Those configurations must not be assumed identical for component-life, material-cycle or maintenance modelling.

## Blocking unknowns

- Full time- and space-resolved source data, diagnostic calibration and reproducible uncertainty treatment.
- Joint temperature, density and energy-confinement evidence sufficient to evaluate the Lawson criterion.
- Fusion yield, driver electrical input, compression energy, energy recovery and full shot-to-shot power balance.
- Repetition rate, component lifetime, outage/maintenance time, consumables, availability and net delivered electricity.
- CAPEX, construction schedule, OPEX, financing terms, decommissioning and independent lifecycle-cost review.

## Unlock criteria and falsifiers

1. Freeze the technical-paper version and source hashes; recover diagnostic calibration and raw or author-supplied data.
2. Reconstruct the reported temperature estimate within the stated uncertainty using the documented diagnostic model.
3. Preregister a held-out-shot prediction for a declared plasma/driver model and compare it with measured data.
4. Measure or source-bound density, confinement, fusion yield, driver input, recovery and net electric conversion at a common boundary.
5. Propagate values into FUS-001 only when their evidence classes and uncertainties are explicit; calculate sensitivity rather than inserting a single optimistic point estimate.
6. Require independent replication and plant-level availability/cost evidence before any commercial or ultra-low-cost claim.

**Stop rule:** if the available evidence remains limited to electron temperature or pulse duration, do not revise net-electricity, LCOE, capacity-factor, WACC or commercialization claims.

## Non-claims

No UQPU project experiment, net electricity, commercial fusion, low-cost fusion, material substitution, accepted bio-oil, financing-rate reduction or Data-Center-to-Phone result is established. INV-030 remains functional material substitution/recovery/recycling/redesign; no elemental transmutation is claimed.
