# Science Discovery Articles

Canonical home for UQPU discovery candidates and verified results in scientific domains outside the dedicated physics and mathematics folders under `INV-037`.

Use `../ARTICLE_TEMPLATE.md` and preserve the evidence-status ladder in `../README.md`.

Examples include chemistry, materials science, energy science, biological or environmental science, manufacturing science and other evidence-based domains relevant to the project.

A model, simulation, uncontrolled observation or project-only reproduction must not be labeled `VERIFIED_DISCOVERY` without the required novelty, uncertainty, falsification and independent-validation gates.
