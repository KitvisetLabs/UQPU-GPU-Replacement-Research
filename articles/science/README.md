# Science Verified-Discovery Articles

Canonical home for **verified genuinely new UQPU scientific discoveries outside the dedicated physics and mathematics folders** under `INV-037`.

Do not place ideas, hypotheses, candidate mechanisms, simulation-only results, project-only reproductions, preliminary observations or literature reviews here. Those remain in ordinary research folders until the full verified-discovery gate passes.

Eligible domains may include chemistry, materials science, energy science, biological or environmental science, manufacturing science and other evidence-based fields relevant to UQPU.

Entry requires documented novelty, reproducibility, falsification/alternative-explanation analysis, uncertainty/error analysis and independent validation appropriate to the field. Use `../ARTICLE_TEMPLATE.md` only after those requirements have been satisfied.
