# PERMANENT PRINCIPLE — IMAGINATION FOR EQUATION INVENTION, KNOWLEDGE FOR TECHNOLOGY

The project shall never treat the present mathematical form of physics as the final endpoint of scientific inquiry. Established equations and experimentally supported physical limits are mandatory constraints on current engineering claims, but the project may continuously ask whether a **better mathematical description, new effective equation, new approximation, new constitutive law, new algorithmic physical model, or ultimately a new physical theory** can explain observations more accurately or open a new technological route.

This does **not** grant permission to ignore established evidence. It creates a disciplined program for inventing and testing alternatives.

The governing distinction is:

> **Imagination is maximally encouraged in proposing new mathematical structures and equation hypotheses. Knowledge, measurement, reproducibility and engineering evidence govern whether those hypotheses may be used to build technology or support a mission claim.**

## Why this is permanent

Scientific equations are historically revisable models of nature. New observations, better mathematics, larger experimental domains and new theoretical frameworks can extend, approximate, unify or replace earlier descriptions within appropriate regimes. Therefore the UQPU / Ketskaew Chulamani research program must continuously monitor new physics, mathematics and scientific-method research rather than freezing its worldview at the equations known on the project start date.

However, a new equation generated by a human or AI is **not a new law of nature merely because it is mathematically elegant or fits one dataset**. Every candidate must pass progressively stronger gates.

## AI role

AI is an active scientific collaborator for:

- symbolic regression and equation search;
- hypothesis and conjecture generation;
- dimensional-analysis and symmetry checks;
- automated algebra, proof search and counterexample search;
- comparison against known limiting cases;
- search over effective models, constitutive laws and reduced-order descriptions;
- experimental-design proposal;
- literature surveillance for new equations, methods, measurements and anomalies;
- maintaining a public GitHub record of promising, failed and falsified paths.

AI is **not** an authority that can promote a generated formula directly to physical truth.

## Equation-discovery ladder

Every candidate should move through as much of this chain as applicable:

```text
mission bottleneck
-> identify current governing model and domain
-> specify what observation/limit is unexplained or costly
-> generate mathematical candidates
-> dimensional / symmetry / conservation / causality checks
-> known-limit and theorem checks
-> fit on training data
-> blind / held-out prediction
-> adversarial counterexample search
-> independent simulation or proof where applicable
-> discriminating experiment
-> independent replication
-> device / algorithm consequence
-> full engineering and economic validation
-> mission-level evidence
```

Failure at any stage is retained as useful negative evidence.

## Equation hypothesis contract

A proposed relation must record at minimum:

1. `equation_id` and version;
2. current accepted model / baseline;
3. proposed symbolic equation or mathematical object;
4. physical variables and units;
5. domain of validity and assumptions;
6. causal or mechanistic interpretation, if claimed;
7. relevant conservation, symmetry, thermodynamic, relativistic, quantum-information and mathematical constraints;
8. scaling behavior and limiting cases;
9. dataset / simulation / proof provenance;
10. falsifier and discriminating observable;
11. baseline predictive error and candidate predictive error on held-out evidence;
12. uncertainty and parameter identifiability;
13. independent validation plan;
14. downstream technology hypothesis;
15. governed evidence label.

## Evidence rule

Use the repository evidence taxonomy. Typical progression is:

`CONCEPT -> THEORY -> MODEL_ONLY -> SIMULATION -> LAB_RESULT / PUBLISHED_EXPERIMENT -> PROTOTYPE -> ...`

An AI-generated expression normally starts at **CONCEPT** or **THEORY**. Symbolic regression that fits existing data remains **MODEL_ONLY** until independent predictive or experimental validation supports it. A statistically better fit alone does not establish a new fundamental law.

## Current physics limits remain binding until displaced by evidence

Landauer bounds, quantum speed limits, thermodynamics, causality, relativity, no-cloning, measurement theory, Standard-Model constraints and other well-supported principles remain active constraints on engineering work. A proposed equation may challenge or extend an established relation only through an explicit experiment/proof that can discriminate between the incumbent model and the candidate.

The project shall never use the phrase "future equations may change" as a shortcut around a present impossibility result. Instead, that impossibility result becomes the specification for the new theory search: **which assumption must change, what new equation changes it, what new observable follows, and how can nature decide?**

## Permanent literature surveillance

Every meaningful research cycle must search recent public literature for advances relevant to equation and law discovery, including:

- symbolic regression / neural-symbolic models;
- AI-assisted mathematical discovery and theorem proving;
- physics-informed machine learning;
- automated experiment design;
- new effective field theories / constitutive relations / governing equations;
- experimentally motivated modifications to established models;
- new constraints, no-go theorems and impossibility bounds.

Material developments are summarized in GitHub with publication date, source, evidence type, what is genuinely new, limitations, and exact relevance to project bottlenecks. Literature summaries do not convert third-party claims into project evidence.

## Initial 2025–2026 literature signals

The research direction is already technically plausible as a scientific-method program:

- **Ruan et al., "Discovering physical laws with parallel symbolic enumeration," Nature Computational Science (published 2025; volume 2026):** scalable symbolic enumeration for interpretable law discovery over synthetic and experimental datasets. https://www.nature.com/articles/s43588-025-00904-8
- **Taskin, Xie & Lazebnik, "Knowledge integration for physics-informed symbolic regression using pre-trained large language models," Scientific Reports (2026):** integrates LLM guidance with symbolic regression while explicitly noting that an LLM is not itself a guarantee of physical understanding. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-35327-6
- **Lazebnik & Liberzon, "Moving from table to graph in physics-informed spatio-temporal symbolic regression," Scientific Reports (2026):** develops equation discovery for spatio-temporal systems governed by ODE/PDE structure. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-53882-w
- **Ying et al., "A neural symbolic model for space physics," Nature Machine Intelligence (2025):** PhyE2E discovers interpretable space-physics formulas from observational data using neural-symbolic methods. DOI: 10.1038/s42256-025-01126-3
- **Google DeepMind, AlphaEvolve (2025) and subsequent mathematical exploration work:** demonstrates LLM-guided evolutionary search coupled to automated evaluators for algorithmic and mathematical discovery. https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/
- **Krenn et al., "Designing physics experiments with artificial intelligence," Nature (2026 review):** surveys AI systems that search experimental configurations and emphasizes simulators, objective functions and constraints as central ingredients. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10898-6

These works demonstrate expanding AI support for scientific discovery; they do **not** demonstrate that AI can arbitrarily rewrite nature's laws or remove any specific physical limit relevant to UQPU.

## Permanent research programs

### EQN-001 — Physics-equation literature watch
Continuously scan new peer-reviewed papers and important preprints. Publish material deltas to GitHub.

### EQN-002 — Symbolic-regression benchmark
Test equation-discovery methods first on known laws with hidden equations, noise, unit constraints and held-out regimes. Measure recovery rate, extrapolation and false-discovery rate.

### EQN-003 — Constraint-aware equation generator
Develop AI-assisted candidate generation that incorporates units, symmetries, conservation laws, causality, positivity/stability and domain-specific constraints before expensive simulation or experiment.

### EQN-004 — Counterexample and falsification engine
For every candidate, automatically search limiting cases, theorem conflicts, dimensional failures and adversarial data regimes.

### EQN-005 — New-physics escalation gate
Only after an established model leaves a reproducible anomaly or mission bottleneck unexplained should the project prioritize genuinely modified fundamental-physics hypotheses. Each must predict a discriminating observable.

### EQN-006 — Equation-to-technology traceability
No candidate contributes to the North Star until a complete chain exists from mathematical relation to physical mechanism, implementable primitive, useful workload/service, manufacturability, energy/material requirements and total cost.

## North-Star relationship

The Data-Center-to-One-Phone target is intentionally extreme. Present equations and known bounds may eventually prove that parts of the target are impossible, or they may reveal enormous engineering headroom. This program keeps both possibilities scientifically productive:

- if an established bound is decisive, record the impossibility and redesign the mission contract;
- if the bound is loose, measure the engineering gap;
- if reproducible observations contradict the current model, develop and test new mathematics;
- if a new validated relation enables a new physical primitive, carry it through device and economic evidence gates.

**The project is open-ended in imagination, but closed to unsupported claims.** That combination is the permanent rule.
