# Permanent rule

Whenever UQPU develops, reproduces, validates, or identifies a materially new result in **physics, science, or mathematics**, the project must create or update a public research article under the root-level `articles/` folder.

The purpose is twofold:

1. preserve every potentially important new result in a visible, searchable, durable publication area rather than allowing it to disappear inside chats, commits or benchmark files;
2. separate imaginative novelty from verified discovery so that the project can explore without overstating evidence.

The canonical folders are:

- `articles/physics/`
- `articles/science/`
- `articles/mathematics/`

The canonical article template is `articles/ARTICLE_TEMPLATE.md`.

# Evidence statuses

Every article must carry one of these statuses:

- `HYPOTHESIS_OR_PROPOSAL`
- `PROJECT_REPRODUCED_RESULT`
- `INDEPENDENTLY_REPRODUCED_CANDIDATE`
- `VERIFIED_DISCOVERY`
- `ESTABLISHED_EXTERNAL_DISCOVERY`

Only `VERIFIED_DISCOVERY` may be described by the repository as a verified new project discovery. `ESTABLISHED_EXTERNAL_DISCOVERY` must credit the external discoverers and is not a UQPU discovery claim.

# Publication trigger

Create/update an article when at least one of the following occurs:

- a new equation/model/mechanism survives a meaningful validation gate;
- a new physical/scientific effect or material/process relationship is reproducibly observed;
- a new mathematical theorem/bound/counterexample/algorithmic result is proved or strongly validated;
- an existing candidate receives independent reproduction or is refuted;
- a literature finding materially changes the novelty status of an existing project hypothesis;
- a negative/no-go result materially narrows the research space.

Early hypotheses may be published immediately, but must use the correct non-discovery status.

# Promotion boundary

Physics/empirical science requires domain-appropriate evidence, uncertainty, falsification and independent reproduction before `VERIFIED_DISCOVERY`.

Mathematics requires a precise theorem statement, assumptions, complete proof/formal artifact, counterexample/boundary checks, novelty review and independent proof checking/review before `VERIFIED_DISCOVERY`.

Simulation, theory, symbolic regression, AI generation, numerical fitting, internal-only reproduction or attractive mathematical structure do not alone establish a verified discovery or new physical law.

# Non-claims

INV-037 does not retroactively classify prior UQPU models/simulations as discoveries and does not weaken existing non-claim boundaries for real-QPU performance, quantum advantage, GPU/NPU/RAM/DRAM/HBM replacement, `>=100x`, `>=100,000,000x`, Data-Center-to-One-Phone, or new-law claims.
