## Strategic meaning

The master plan is not only a sequence of industries. It is also a sequence of **knowledge depth**. When a technology bottleneck cannot be solved economically at the current engineering layer, the program may invest upstream into deeper mathematics, information theory, quantum foundations, condensed-matter/AMO physics, QFT/particle physics, chemistry/materials or other sciences, then return downstream with a measurable mechanism.

The investment thesis is:

```text
cheapest decisive theorem / simulation / experiment
-> reduce uncertainty about a fundamental mechanism
-> avoid capital-intensive dead ends
-> identify a scalable primitive or impossibility bound
-> translate to device/algorithm/material/energy design
-> lower required CAPEX/OPEX/time-to-evidence
-> lower financed principal / absolute interest burden
-> reinvest into the next evidence gate
```

This means foundational science is not separated from INV-033 financing strategy. A proof that a route is impossible can be financially valuable because it prevents later factory/device capital from being committed to an invalid mechanism.

## Capital rule for deep science

No large capital escalation follows from the depth or excitement of a hypothesis alone. Each frontier project should be funded in the following order:

1. mathematical consistency and known-constraint review;
2. cheapest analytical bound or counterexample;
3. smallest discriminating simulation;
4. smallest discriminating experiment;
5. reproducibility and independent verification;
6. device-level scaling study;
7. manufacturing and reliability study;
8. economic and financing model;
9. larger prototype/capital only when the evidence gate is passed.

## Strategic domains

Lane H should continuously scan foundational research for causal routes into:
- computation, AI and optimization;
- RAM/VRAM/storage/state semantics;
- photonics, semiconductors and quantum devices;
- critical-material reduction and biomass-carbon materials;
- bio-oil conversion/catalysis;
- fusion/plasma/nuclear/materials physics;
- energy conversion and thermodynamics;
- sensing/metrology/manufacturing;
- data-center-to-phone physical compression.

## Evidence boundary

Particle physics, QFT, quantum gravity, unknown particles/fields, vacuum effects or other speculative mechanisms are not strategic assets merely because they are fundamental. They become actionable only when a mathematical/physical mechanism, known-constraint analysis, observable and falsifying experiment connect them to a project function.

The project therefore seeks maximum depth **without weakening scientific skepticism**.
