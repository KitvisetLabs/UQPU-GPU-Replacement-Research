## Objective

UQPU shall investigate whether **any experimentally established or mathematically serious fundamental-particle / quantum-field-theory framework can yield a useful technology primitive**, directly or indirectly, for computation, AI training/inference, memory/state handling, sensing, communication, materials, energy, control or system-cost compression.

The search is intentionally open-ended. It is not restricted to conventional qubit hardware and is not restricted to one currently fashionable theory.

## Mandatory coverage families

The research registry shall include, and remain open to extensions beyond:

1. Standard Model quantum field theory;
2. QCD: up/down/charm/strange/top/bottom quarks, gluons, colour, confinement, hadrons and strongly coupled QCD phases;
3. QED and electromagnetic quantum fields;
4. electroweak interactions, W/Z sectors and weak processes;
5. Higgs/Yukawa-sector structures;
6. neutrino flavour, mixing and weakly interacting information/sensing hypotheses;
7. lattice gauge theories and effective field theories;
8. strongly coupled/exotic matter, exotic hadrons and quark-gluon-plasma-related mechanisms where a technology chain can be stated;
9. falsifiable beyond-Standard-Model proposals, including axion/ALP, dark-sector, sterile-state, supersymmetry, compositeness, unification, extra-dimension and related proposals without assuming any are true;
10. string theory, M-theory and holography, first as mathematical/information structures and only as physical-device routes if an experimentally supported mechanism is identified;
11. other quantum-gravity programs such as loop/spinfoam, causal-set/dynamical-triangulation, asymptotic-safety, emergent-gravity, noncommutative and other mathematically explicit approaches;
12. newly published or newly invented fundamental theories that satisfy the project's falsifiability and evidence rules.

"All theories" is implemented as a **coverage mandate**, not a statement that all theories are correct or equally supported.

## Quark route — direct and indirect

Up and down quarks do have distinct quantum numbers and participate differently in hadronic structure. This difference can motivate technology hypotheses, but the project must not jump from "different states exist" to "a quark transistor or quark bit is practical."

The immediate known obstacle is confinement: ordinary low-energy experiments do not provide stable isolated colour-charged quarks that can simply be addressed like semiconductor logic states. Therefore the search must evaluate both:

- **direct routes:** only where preparation, controllability, retention and readout of the proposed degree of freedom are physically specified;
- **indirect routes:** hadronic/nuclear states, QCD-derived or QCD-analogue phases, symmetry structures, gauge-theory simulation, sensors, transducers, materials consequences and algorithms inspired by the field theory.

A direct route that fails can still produce an indirect technology result.

## String / holography route

String theory and related quantum-gravity frameworks are admitted as high-risk exploratory routes. Current inclusion does **not** imply an experimentally verified string-scale computing device.

Near-term technology hypotheses should first examine mathematical or information-theoretic structures—duality, holography, tensor-network/error-correction relations, complexity geometry, encoding and algorithmic transformations—and then ask whether those structures reduce resources after mapping back to realizable hardware and full input/output accounting.

Physical-device claims require a separately evidenced measurable mechanism.

## Mandatory promotion contract

Every concrete candidate must state:

`mathematical formulation -> causal physical mechanism -> target bottleneck -> known constraints -> scaling law -> falsifier -> observable -> baseline -> full resource accounting -> evidence label`.

No candidate is promoted because of novelty alone. Negative results are retained.

## Initial evidence map

- CERN's Standard Model summary remains the baseline for quark/lepton/gauge/Higgs sector classification.
- 2025–2026 literature shows active experimentally grounded work on quantum simulation of gauge theories and fermionic systems, providing a concrete indirect bridge from particle/QFT structure to controllable quantum hardware.
- String/holography literature provides rich quantum-information structures, but this batch does not identify an experimentally established string-theory hardware primitive.

## Non-claims

This objective does **not** demonstrate a direct quark computer, string-theory computer, new elementary particle, beyond-Standard-Model discovery, new physical law, quantum advantage, GPU/NPU/RAM/DRAM/HBM replacement, 100,000,000× cost reduction, or the Data-Center-to-One-Phone target.
