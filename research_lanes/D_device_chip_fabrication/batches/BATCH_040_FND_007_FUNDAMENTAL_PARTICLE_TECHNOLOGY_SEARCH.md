## Objective

UQPU shall investigate whether **elementary particles and their experimentally established or mathematically serious theoretical descriptions can yield useful technology primitives**, directly or indirectly, for computation, AI training/inference, memory/state handling, sensing, communication, materials, energy, control or system-cost compression.

The research target is the **elementary particles themselves and particle-level degrees of freedom**. QCD, QED, electroweak theory, quantum field theory, string theory and other frameworks are included as tools for describing, predicting or inventing testable particle-level technology routes; they are not substitutes for the elementary-particle objective.

The search is intentionally open-ended. It is not restricted to conventional qubit hardware and is not restricted to one currently fashionable theory.

## Mandatory elementary-particle coverage

The candidate registry shall include all established Standard Model elementary-particle families and remain open to experimentally testable proposed particles:

1. quarks: up, down, charm, strange, top and bottom;
2. charged leptons: electron, muon and tau;
3. neutrinos: electron, muon and tau flavour/mass-state physics;
4. gauge bosons: photon, gluon, W and Z;
5. Higgs boson and Higgs/Yukawa-sector particle-level effects;
6. antiparticles and particle/antiparticle state structures where technologically meaningful;
7. proposed elementary particles from falsifiable beyond-Standard-Model frameworks, including axion/ALP, dark-sector, sterile-neutrino, supersymmetric, compositeness, extra-dimensional or other candidates, without assuming their existence;
8. any newly discovered or newly proposed elementary particle that can satisfy the project's evidence and falsifiability gates.

The theories used to study these particles may include Standard Model QFT, QCD, QED, electroweak theory, lattice gauge theory, effective field theory, string/M-theory, holography and other quantum-gravity/BSM frameworks. "Include all" is a **coverage mandate**, not a statement that all speculative theories or particles are true or equally supported.

## Quark route — direct and indirect

Up and down quarks have distinct quantum numbers and participate differently in hadronic structure. This difference can motivate technology hypotheses, but the project must not jump from "different states exist" to "a quark transistor or quark bit is practical."

The immediate known obstacle is confinement: ordinary low-energy experiments do not provide stable isolated colour-charged quarks that can simply be addressed like semiconductor logic states. Therefore the search must evaluate both:

- **direct routes:** only where preparation, controllability, retention and readout of the proposed particle degree of freedom are physically specified;
- **indirect routes:** hadronic/nuclear states, QCD-derived or QCD-analogue phases, symmetry structures, gauge-theory simulation, sensors, transducers, materials consequences and algorithms inspired by particle physics.

A direct route that fails can still produce an indirect technology result.

## String / holography route

String theory and related quantum-gravity frameworks are admitted as high-risk exploratory **descriptions or generators of particle-level hypotheses**. Current inclusion does **not** imply an experimentally verified string-scale particle or computing device.

Near-term technology hypotheses should first examine mathematical or information-theoretic structures—duality, holography, tensor-network/error-correction relations, complexity geometry, encoding and algorithmic transformations—and then ask whether those structures predict a particle-level mechanism or reduce resources after mapping back to realizable hardware and full input/output accounting.

Physical-device claims require a separately evidenced measurable particle/state mechanism.

## Mandatory promotion contract

Every concrete elementary-particle technology candidate must state:

`particle / state -> mathematical formulation -> causal physical mechanism -> preparation -> controllability -> retention/coherence -> readout -> target bottleneck -> known constraints -> scaling law -> falsifier -> observable -> baseline -> full resource accounting -> evidence label`.

No candidate is promoted because of novelty alone. Negative results are retained.

## Initial evidence map and literature provenance

1. **Established particle inventory:** CERN's Standard Model overview remains the baseline for quarks, leptons and force-carrier/Higgs sectors: https://home.cern/science/physics/standard-model/
2. **Experimentally grounded indirect bridge:** Meth et al., *Simulating two-dimensional lattice gauge theories on a qudit quantum computer*, Nature Physics 21, 570–576 (2025), experimentally simulates 2D lattice QED with matter and gauge fields on a trapped-ion qudit processor and reports reduced register/circuit complexity for the demonstrated encoding: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-025-02797-w
3. **Broader gauge-theory technology bridge:** Halimeh et al., *Cold-atom quantum simulators of gauge theories*, Nature Physics 21, 25–36 (2025), reviews tunable tabletop quantum simulation of gauge theories and the connection to Standard-Model-type gauge structures: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-024-02721-8
4. **Non-Abelian constraint engineering:** Mathew and Raychowdhury, *Protecting gauge symmetries in the dynamics of SU(3) lattice gauge theories*, Communications Physics 8, 313 (2025), studies maintaining physical gauge constraints under dynamics/noise: https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-025-02230-x
5. **String/holography:** these frameworks remain admitted as theory/information-structure routes, but this batch does not identify an experimentally established string-scale elementary-particle technology primitive.

The highest-information-gain near-term path is therefore **not** to assume isolated quark logic. It is to use experimentally controllable systems that encode elementary-particle/gauge-theory structure, then ask whether any resulting representation/control primitive produces a reproducible resource improvement that can climb from F4 to F7–F10.

## Non-claims

This objective does **not** demonstrate a direct quark computer, elementary-particle computer, string-theory computer, new elementary particle, beyond-Standard-Model discovery, new physical law, quantum advantage, GPU/NPU/RAM/DRAM/HBM replacement, 100,000,000× cost reduction, or the Data-Center-to-One-Phone target.
