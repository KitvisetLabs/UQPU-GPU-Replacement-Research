## Research-owner direction

The project shall expand elementary-particle technology research beyond the current lattice-QED/qudit route to include every scientifically serious difference or degree of freedom at comparable or deeper scales that might become a useful technology primitive.

The owner identifies the general innovation heuristic:

> A technology can emerge from a controllable difference. Binary `0/1` is the simplest example. Search for analogous useful differences in particle spin, particle/antiparticle states, matter/antimatter behavior, fields, energy levels, spacetime/gravity, dark-sector observables, and materials.

The scientific implementation is deliberately stricter than the heuristic:

```text
ΔX -> distinguishability -> preparation -> control -> retention -> readout
   -> useful function -> scaling -> resources -> falsifier -> reproduction
```

For an energy difference use `ΔE = E2 - E1`; the batch does not declare every generic difference to be energy.

## Coverage expansion

### 1. Quark spin / gluon spin / hadronic structure

Brookhaven's Electron-Ion Collider program explicitly targets how quarks, gluons and orbital motion contribute to proton spin and emphasizes quark/gluon confinement. This creates a legitimate research basis for **spin-dependent QCD observables and indirect technology primitives**, while simultaneously blocking a casual claim that isolated quarks are presently addressable logic states.

Near-term technology questions:

- can QCD/hadronic spin structure motivate encodings, sensors or control principles with measurable benefit?
- can gauge-symmetry protection or analog/digital simulation produce a transferable error-suppression primitive?
- can any proposed quark-spin route state a preparation/control/readout mechanism that survives confinement?

Primary anchor: https://www.bnl.gov/eic/goals.php

### 2. Antimatter / antiparticles

Antimatter is experimentally real and controllable only under demanding conditions. CERN ALPHA produces, traps and studies antihydrogen; CERN reported substantially faster antihydrogen production in 2025. These results justify a technology-enabler program around trapping, precision spectroscopy, interferometry, detector/readout systems and matter/antimatter comparison.

They do **not** justify assuming cheap antimatter, net-positive energy generation or a practical antimatter computer.

Anchors:
- https://home.cern/science/experiments/alpha/
- https://home.cern/breakthrough-antimatter-production/

### 3. Dark matter

Dark matter has strong astrophysical/cosmological evidence but its microscopic identity remains unresolved. The technology route is therefore currently **detector-first**, not material-first.

Berkeley Lab explicitly develops superconducting quantum sensors and related devices for dark-matter searches; the 2025 Particle Data Group review discusses quantum-sensor routes for light dark matter.

Research questions:

- can sensor technologies developed for dark-matter searches create useful low-energy detection, phonon, microwave or materials capabilities independent of discovery?
- which dark-matter model predicts a measurable coupling accessible to a controlled sensor?
- what experimental null result excludes the candidate parameter region?

Anchors:
- https://www.physics.lbl.gov/qiscom/qis-for-hep/
- https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-dark-matter.pdf

### 4. Dark energy / cosmic acceleration

DESI DR2 provides high-precision cosmological constraints and strengthened evidence that motivates testing evolving-dark-energy models, but dark energy is not an experimentally localized controllable fuel or device medium.

Technology research here must therefore focus on instrumentation, precision spectroscopy, detectors, timing, inference, large-scale mapping and mathematical/algorithmic structures unless a future experiment establishes a local controllable mechanism.

Anchor: https://www.desi.lbl.gov/2025/03/19/desi-dr2-results-march-19-guide/

### 5. Spacetime / gravity / relativistic differences

This route already has a direct technology bridge. NIST documents atomic clocks as quantum sensors of gravitational potential and discusses geodesy, subsurface sensing and tests of relativity. The relevant difference is often frequency/time accumulation under different gravitational potentials.

Candidate technology primitives include:

- clock-based gravimetry/geodesy;
- distributed time/frequency sensing;
- inertial/interferometric sensing;
- navigation and synchronization;
- precision tests that constrain new interactions or dark-sector couplings.

Anchor: https://www.nist.gov/quantum-information-science/quantum-sensing-explained/atomic-clocks-exquisite-sensors-more-than-just-time

### 6. Extra dimensions / quantum gravity / vacuum structure

These remain high-risk theory programs. A candidate can enter the registry only if it specifies a discriminating observable, measurement architecture and falsifier. No “extra-dimensional device” language is permitted from mathematical structure alone.

### 7. Diamond spin-defect bridge

Diamond NV and other color centers are experimentally grounded examples of a useful microscopic difference: discrete spin/optical states can be prepared, manipulated and read out, enabling sensing and quantum-information research.

This route is strategically important because it can couple directly to the project's Pangola/biomass-carbon program.

Anchors:
- https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-024-00775-4
- https://doi.org/10.1038/s44172-025-00398-2

### 8. Other particle-scale routes

The registry remains open to neutrinos, muons, nuclear/hadronic states, photons, bosons, topological/quasiparticle states and newly discovered or proposed particles, provided evidence level and direct-vs-indirect status are explicit.

## Pangola/Biomass -> Synthetic Diamond starting hypothesis

The project shall test whether carbon-rich material recovered from Pangola grass and agricultural-residue bio-oil/thermochemical processes can be converted into a sufficiently pure precursor for synthetic diamond and, eventually, defect-engineered diamond technology.

```text
biomass feedstock
-> conversion + carbon-rich residue/coproduct
-> purification / ash-metal removal
-> precursor conditioning / graphitization or hydrocarbon route
-> HPHT or CVD diamond experiment
-> Raman/XRD/phase + crystal-quality verification
-> defect engineering
-> ODMR / optical / microwave state readout
-> coherence/sensitivity/device metric
-> lifecycle cost and energy
```

Existing literature supports biomass-derived engineered carbons and synthetic-diamond growth from suitable carbon/hydrocarbon precursors, but this exact Pangola-residue-to-device chain is **not demonstrated**. It begins as `MATERIAL_PATH_HYPOTHESIS`.

## Promotion contract

Every candidate must provide:

`domain -> difference_coordinate -> evidence_level -> carrier_status -> preparation -> control -> retention -> readout -> useful_function -> baseline -> resources -> falsifier -> evidence_label`.

Additional rules:

- a detector technology is not automatically a controllable technology made from the detected phenomenon;
- a cosmological inference is not an extractable energy source;
- a theoretical extra dimension is not a device channel;
- a synthetic-diamond route must prove the actual feedstock/process/material chain;
- direct quark technology must solve the confinement/preparation/control/readout problem;
- antimatter energy accounting must include production, trapping, cooling, losses and annihilation handling.

## Non-claims

This batch does not demonstrate dark matter, dark energy or extra dimensions as controllable technology media; does not demonstrate a quark computer, antimatter computer or antimatter energy source; does not demonstrate Pangola-derived device-grade diamond; and does not establish a new physical law, quantum advantage, subsystem replacement or extraordinary cost saving.

The publishable result is the research architecture itself: the elementary-particle program now has an explicit difference-to-technology principle, mandatory particle/spacetime coverage, a biomass-to-diamond material bridge and falsifiable promotion rules.
