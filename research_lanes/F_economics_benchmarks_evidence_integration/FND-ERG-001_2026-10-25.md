## Objective

Evaluate whether a confidence-certified lower bound on extractable work from partial quantum-state information can be reproduced as a useful *state-resource* field in future thermodynamic accounting, while preventing confusion with the energy consumed by a UQPU workload.

Primary source: Pagliaro et al., “Certifying ergotropy under partial information,” *Physical Review Letters* 137, 130401 (2026-09-21), DOI 10.1103/pl26-g6vy; arXiv v2 revised 2026-09-28, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18828.

## Mathematical object and assumptions

For state rho and known Hamiltonian H, ergotropy is the maximum average work extractable by a unitary:
  
  E(rho,H) = max_U [Tr(rho H) - Tr(H U rho U†)].

The paper proposes an SDP-based lower certificate from a feasible set of states consistent with measured observable expectations. With finite shots, confidence intervals widen the constraints; for observables bounded in [-1,1], the paper uses epsilon_i = sqrt(2 log(2K/delta) / N_i), where K is the number of observables, N_i is the shots for observable i, and 1-delta is the requested confidence level.

Assumptions to preserve: known Hamiltonian; correct observable definitions and normalization; valid finite-sample confidence model; state positivity and unit trace; numerical SDP feasibility/accuracy. Hamiltonian uncertainty, calibration drift, correlated samples and model mismatch must not be silently ignored.

## Required outputs

- Frozen paper/version, equations and method transcription.
- Small exact-state fixtures with known rho and H, plus passive-state, noisy-state, rank-deficient and information-poor negative controls.
- For every fixture: exact ergotropy, certified lower bound, observable set, shots per observable, confidence delta, SDP solver/status/residuals, and whether the bound is trivial or nontrivial.
- Repeated finite-shot trials with preregistered empirical coverage test; retain all failed candidates and seeds.
- Adversarial checks for invalid observables, normalization/sign errors, non-finite values, changed Hamiltonian, dependent measurements and solver tolerance.
- Independent reproduction before any optional provider experiment.

## Acceptance and falsification

1. The lower certificate must not exceed exact ergotropy beyond the predeclared numerical tolerance.
2. The declared confidence procedure must pass the preregistered coverage criterion across repeated draws; report uncertainty and sample count.
3. Adding constraints must preserve monotonicity only under the paper's retained-unitary safeguard; do not assume a naive re-optimization is automatically monotone.
4. An information-poor case may validly return a zero certificate. Do not loosen the gate to force a positive result.
5. Any bound violation, failed coverage test, invalid-feasible-set acceptance or unreproducible solver result is a retained negative result and blocks promotion.

## Separate system-energy ledger

Do not treat ergotropy as processor energy consumption. Maintain distinct fields for:
- certified extractable-work lower bound and confidence;
- metered energy per accepted useful task, with declared host/QPU/control/network/cooling boundary;
- ideal physical lower bound only when operation counts and physical assumptions are independently justified;
- any gap metric only when units, time interval and system boundaries are compatible.

No subtraction of ergotropy from facility electricity, and no claim of improved energy efficiency, without a physically consistent boundary and matched useful-output CPU/GPU/NPU baseline.
