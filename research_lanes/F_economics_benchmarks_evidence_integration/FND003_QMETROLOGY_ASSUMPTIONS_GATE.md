# FND-003-QMETROLOGY-001 — estimation-target assumptions and matched benchmark contract

**Primary owner:** Lane F — economics / benchmarks / evidence / integration

**Source:** Pienaar, Tal & Ciftci, arXiv:2610.31632 (2026-10-22), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31632

## Purpose

Prevent a benchmark from confusing an operational estimate with an ontological claim that a quantity had a unique, pre-existing value independent of measurement. The source preprint's no-go theorem concerns a set of assumptions when the target is modeled as a quantum variable and probe-target entanglement plus state completeness are retained. It is not a prohibition on practical quantum metrology or on measuring classical latency/energy parameters.

## Required record for each FND-003 comparison

1. Define the quantity and task boundary: classical parameter or quantum observable/variable.
2. Record probe, target, apparatus, estimator, measurement operators, calibration snapshot, repeat count, uncertainty/confidence method and raw data.
3. State whether probe-target entanglement is relevant, what source degrees of freedom are accessible, and whether decoherence/coarse-graining justifies an effective classical parameter.
4. Match the workload, target identity, control, accepted-output quality, latency, energy and cost boundary before comparing quantum and classical systems.
5. Distinguish a statistical estimate from a claim about a pre-existing true value. Do not use the preprint to discard operationally reproducible engineering measurements.
6. If an alternative physical measurement model is proposed, identify the incumbent assumption, replacement dynamics, discriminating observable, falsifier, held-out prediction, experiment and independent replication.

## Falsifiers and limits

The scope guard is falsified as relevant to a specific benchmark if the target is demonstrably in a classical-parameter regime and all operational predictions agree; then there is no engineering consequence. If a quantum-variable regime is claimed, a useful follow-up must show an observable difference beyond interpretation alone. A theorem about incompatible assumptions cannot be turned into an energy lower bound, speedup claim, or new physical law.
