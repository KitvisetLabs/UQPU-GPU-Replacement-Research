# Lane G — Quantum Chip Manufacturing Equipment & Software

## Scope
Manufacturing machines and factory software: DUV/EUV/alternatives, optics/source/masks, deposition, etch, implantation/doping where applicable, cleaning, wafer handling, metrology/inspection, process control, EDA/TCAD/process simulation, robotics/automation, cryogenic/electrical/optical characterization, bonding, assembly, packaging and test.

## Integration responsibilities
G continuously co-designs with D and exposes measurable process-window, equipment, throughput, yield and cost constraints to E/F/H while taking functional requirements from A-C.

Future equipment/process/factory-software subfolders are allowed subject to the 16-level depth limit.
