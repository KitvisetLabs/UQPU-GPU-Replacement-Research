# Lane D contract — molecular spin–photon device acceptance

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Primary owner:** Lane D; co-design Lane G  
**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11147-6  
**Status:** EXTERNAL EXPERIMENT / UQPU REPRODUCTION NOT RUN

The function under test is an individually addressable molecular spin with optical excitation/readout and spin-selective optical transitions. A crystal sample is not itself a network node, processor or useful computing service.

1. Pin the paper, source data and protocol; record access/license and available code revision.
2. Independently reproduce single-emitter signature, optical stability/linewidth, spin-selective transitions, Rabi response, coherence and relaxation with uncertainty.
3. Repeat independently prepared batches; report doping uniformity, morphology, activation yield and photobleaching.
4. Test substrate transfer/integration; measure before/after changes in coherence, linewidth, photon yield and stability.
5. Measure temperature, optical collection, control/readout energy, cryogenic load, packaging volume, failure/replacement and cost per accepted spin–photon operation.
6. Compare against a matched atomic-defect baseline under the same device boundary.
7. Require independent replication before Lane G scale-up or Lane B provider/device mapping.

The paper reports a cryogenic single-molecule experiment, not wafer-scale manufacturing, mass production, provider access, a complete quantum computer, material substitution or cost advantage.
