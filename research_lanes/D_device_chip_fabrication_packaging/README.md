# Lane D — Quantum / Photonic / Semiconductor Devices, Chip Architecture, Fabrication & Packaging

## Scope
Device physics, quantum/photonic/semiconductor chip architecture, process architecture, fabrication requirements, packaging and manufacturability.

Lane D also owns **FUND-PARTICLE**, the elementary-particle-to-technology research program. Its primary research objects are elementary particles and particle-specific quantum properties: quark flavors, charged leptons, neutrino flavors, photon/gluon/W/Z, Higgs, and explicitly labeled hypothetical particle candidates. Fundamental theories such as QED, QCD, electroweak QFT, lattice gauge theory or string-inspired models are used as explanatory/design tools when they provide a falsifiable bridge from a particle degree of freedom to a controllable observable or device/simulator abstraction.

The operating rule is **particle first; direct if possible, indirect if necessary**. Directly inaccessible particles must not be promoted to device claims merely because their dynamics can be simulated. Valid indirect routes may proceed through bound states, isospin/nuclear states, collective excitations or quantum simulators provided the evidence class remains explicit.
