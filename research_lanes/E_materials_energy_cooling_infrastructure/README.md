# Lane E — Biomass / Advanced Materials / Energy / Cooling / Infrastructure

## Scope
Biomass-derived functional materials, advanced materials, energy systems, cooling, facilities and infrastructure that can enable or lower total system cost.

## Integration responsibilities
E starts from functional requirements supplied by A-D/F/G rather than raw feedstock price alone. Results feed D/G design and qualification, F lifecycle economics, and H development strategy. Ordinary chemistry is never treated as elemental transmutation.

Future materials/energy/cooling/facility subfolders are allowed subject to the 16-level depth limit.
