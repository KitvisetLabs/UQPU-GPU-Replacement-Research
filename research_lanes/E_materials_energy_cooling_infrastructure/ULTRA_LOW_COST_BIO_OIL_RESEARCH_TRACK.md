## Primary measurable contract

For each feedstock/process route record:

```text
feedstock identity + dry composition + moisture + ash
-> preprocessing energy/cost
-> conversion mass/energy balance
-> crude liquid yield + quality
-> stabilization/upgrading inputs + losses
-> final saleable liquid quality + LHV
-> final saleable liters/GJ per dry tonne
-> CAPEX/OPEX/logistics/financing
-> cost per accepted liter and cost per GJ
```

Raw condensate yield is not the acceptance metric.

## Initial route matrix

| Route | Potential reason to study | Main cost risk | Evidence needed next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fast pyrolysis | high liquid-intermediate production rate | oxygenated/unstable oil, drying, upgrading | Pangola mass/energy balance + oil assay |
| Catalytic fast pyrolysis | reduce later upgrading burden | catalyst cost/deactivation, carbon loss | catalyst lifetime + saleable-carbon yield |
| Hydrothermal liquefaction | can avoid full drying of wet feedstocks | pressure equipment/CAPEX, aqueous processing | wet-feed energy balance + product quality |
| Gasification -> liquids | flexible syngas platform | plant complexity and scale | full CAPEX/yield comparison |
| Refinery co-processing | reuse existing upgrading assets | compatibility, contaminants, refinery constraints | accepted blend/feedstock test + avoided CAPEX |

## Pangola-specific unknowns

No Pangola route is currently certified as lowest cost. Measure seasonal delivered dry-biomass cost, ash/mineral chemistry, moisture, size-reduction energy, liquid/char/gas yields, oil oxygen/water/acidity/stability/heating value, hydrogen requirement and final saleable-fuel yield.

## Integration with INV-030

Char/carbon coproducts may enter the advanced-material program, but coproduct credits must be market- and quality-qualified and never double counted between fuel and materials economics.
