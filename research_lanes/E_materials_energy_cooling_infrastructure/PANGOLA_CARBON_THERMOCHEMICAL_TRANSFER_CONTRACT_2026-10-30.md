## Input contract

- Identified Pangola/agricultural-residue lot, source and sampling plan.
- Dry-basis moisture, ash, elemental composition and trace-contaminant assay.
- One named target function, target component, reference material and acceptance envelope.
- Frozen process recipe and mass/energy/catalyst/waste accounting.

## Output contract

Report accepted functional units per dry tonne of feedstock, property distributions with uncertainty, batch-to-batch variation, durability, reject rate, catalyst recovery, utility consumption and lifecycle cost per accepted functional unit. A material can pass one function and fail another; do not use “advanced carbon” as a universal acceptance label.

## Required controls

1. Untreated feedstock.
2. Catalyst-free HTC/carbonization process control.
3. Catalyst-assisted candidate only with declared catalyst loading/recovery.
4. Commercial/reference material under the same measurement method.
5. Independent batch replication and blinded sample labels where practical.

## Evidence and non-claims

The woodchip TEA/LCA and spruce-bark graphitization paper are external literature only. Their prices, yields, Raman ratios and conductivity are not Pangola results. No UQPU lab result, device-grade material, universal Au/Cu/Ag/Li/Co/Ni/rare-earth replacement, elemental transmutation, commercial advantage or new physical law is claimed.

## Promotion gate

Promote only after: traceable feedstock and process; target-specific property pass; contamination/stability pass; independent replication; complete material/energy balance; lifecycle and financing boundary; and an independently checked cost comparison. Failure or inconclusive results remain in the gap ledger.
