## Mission and immutable constraints
Research a passenger vehicle and motorcycle that travel with **no part touching the ground**, low acoustic noise, high useful thrust, and minimum full-system energy. Explore Earth-field magnetic coupling, vehicle-mounted superconductors, electromagnetic/gravitational coupling, and credible alternative contactless lift mechanisms. This is a ninth **parallel** lane I; do not replace or suspend A–H. INV-025–035 and all existing root directives, six-language master-plan references, model and funding references remain binding. ## Physics feasibility screen (mandatory first gate)
Earth magnetic field is weak and nonuniform; a vehicle-mounted magnet/superconductor cannot assume a universal stationary reaction rail. Uniform external magnetic fields do not yield arbitrary translational lift; magnetic-gradient force, magnetic susceptibility, available reaction momentum, geometry, field maps, stability and losses must be quantified. Levitation above arbitrary nonmagnetic terrain without an external guideway is **not demonstrated** by adding a superconductor. Gravitational force is not electromagnetism; no established controllable anti-gravity effect is available. Never present speculative gravitational coupling as an engineering fact. Analyze reaction paths for all concepts: guideway, atmosphere (rotor/fan/jet), expelled reaction mass, or externally sourced field gradients. No free thrust, no perpetual motion.
